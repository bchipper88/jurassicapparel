## Three ways our dinosaur socks are made

The prints get all the attention, but there are really three different socks in the range.
The listing tells you which one you're looking at.

**1. One-size "Jurassic Socks" (most of the range).** A 200-needle knit with half-terry
cushioned bottoms and a fleece lining underfoot. Mostly polyester with nylon and a little
stretch; the exact blend varies slightly by design. One size fits women's US 5 up to men's US
12. [T-Rex Skeleton](/products/t-rex-skeleton-socks), [X-Ray](/products/black-skeleton-dinosaur-socks),
and Santa Hat are all made this way.

**2. Fully cushioned crew socks.** Also one size (women's 5 to men's 12) and 200-needle knit,
but cushioned all the way from heel to toe, not just the sole. The blend is 73% polyester,
18% nylon, 6% spandex, 3% cotton. [Dino Nuggies](/products/dino-nuggies-dinosaur-socks) and
[Forest Raptor](/products/forest-raptor-dinosaur-socks) are this kind. If the person you're
buying for lives in boots or stands all day, this is the one.

**3. Sized sublimation socks (S, M, L).** A ribbed tube with cushioned bottoms, printed with a
flat sublimation method. These come in three sizes, which matters if someone's feet fall
outside the one-size range. One honest note from the listing itself: because of how they're
printed, you can see a slight colour difference along the side seams, more so on dark
prints. [Multi-Color Dinos](/products/multi-color-dinos-dinosaur-socks) and
[Pink and Yellow Brontosaurus](/products/pink-and-yellow-brontosaurus-womens-dinosaur-socks)
are this kind.

## How dinosaur socks sizing works

Most of the range is one size, and the listings put it at **women's US 5 to men's US 12**.
That covers most adults and many teens, which is why one-size socks are the safe gift when
you can't ask.

The Pink and Yellow Brontosaurus listing gives this chart for S, M and L:

| Size | Women's US | Men's US | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| S | 6–8 | 5–6.5 | 13 in |
| M | 8.5–11 | 7–9.5 | 15.4 in |
| L | 11.5–13 | 10–13 | 16.2 in |

So if he's a men's 13, pick a sized pair in L. If she's a women's 12, the same.

**One thing these aren't: kids' socks.** The smallest fit we list is women's 5, which is
bigger than most young children's feet.

## When to order dinosaur socks for Christmas

Every pair is printed when you order it, so plan around the listing estimates:

- **One-size Jurassic Socks:** 4–5 business days to make, then 2–5 to ship. Up to 10
  business days.
- **Fully cushioned crew socks:** 2–5 to make, then 3–6 to ship. Up to 11 business days.
- **Sized S/M/L socks:** their listings don't give a shipping time, so order these first.

Working back from the slowest estimate, **order by Monday, December 7** for socks to arrive
by December 22. Earlier is better: the slow end of an estimate is the one that matters in
December. The listings that state shipping give US addresses only.

## Dinosaur socks FAQ

**Are dinosaur socks one size fits all?**
Most of ours are one size, listed as fitting women's US 5 to men's US 12. A few designs come
in S, M and L for smaller or larger feet.

**Do you make dinosaur socks for kids?**
Not at the moment. The smallest size we list starts at a women's 5.

**What are they made of?**
Mostly polyester with nylon and spandex for stretch. The one-size pairs have a fleece-lined,
cushioned sole; the crew pairs are cushioned heel to toe and include 3% cotton.

**Is there a Christmas pair?**
Yes: [Santa Hat](/products/santa-hat-christmas-dinosaur-socks), one size, $19.99.

**Do you ship outside the US?**
The sock listings that state shipping give US shipping only.

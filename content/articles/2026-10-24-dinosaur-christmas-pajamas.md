everyone is actually in the same print. Our dinosaur Christmas pajamas are one original pattern,

pattern hoodie, leggings for kids and big kids, and a bodysuit for the baby. Below is what each piece
is, the sizes and prices from each listing, one honest note about the kids' pieces, and the dates to
order by for Christmas.

## The print

Cozy Christmas Dinos is a whole holiday party in one pattern: a Santa-hat T-Rex, a stegosaurus in a
scarf, a long-neck brontosaurus and a baby dino hatching from its egg, with wrapped gifts, holly,
stars and snowflakes on a deep evergreen background. It's printed edge to edge on everything except
the baby bodysuit, which carries a mid-chest design (the T-Rex and the hatchling) from the same
artwork. Everything is together in the

## Dinosaur Christmas pajamas for adults

**The pajama pants** are the piece most people start with. The
[Cozy Christmas Dinos adult pajama pants](/products/cozy-christmas-dinos-adult-dinosaur-christmas-pajama-pants)
are a relaxed, unisex wide leg in a 93% polyester / 7% elastane mid-weight jersey, with deep side
pockets and an elastic waistband with a drawstring. Jersey with stretch feels closer to good
sweatpants than to thin woven pajama cotton: warmer, softer, and the print doesn't break up at the
seams. Sizes run 2XS to 6XL, and the chart goes by body measurement, from a 28⅜″ waist at 2XS to a
55⅛″ waist at 6XL. Measure your waist and hips and pick the size that matches both. Our
[guide to dinosaur pajamas for adults](/blogs/blog/dinosaur-pajamas-for-adults) goes deeper on fitting
this cut.

**The women's set** is a different fabric for a different feel. The
[women's pajama top](/products/cozy-christmas-dinos-womens-dinosaur-christmas-pajama-top) is a
silky, satin-feel button-up with long sleeves, a chest pocket and contrast piping on the collar,
pocket and cuffs. The matching
[women's pajama pants](/products/cozy-christmas-dinos-womens-dinosaur-christmas-pajama-pants) are
straight-leg with piping down the side seams. Both are lightweight 100% polyester, in XS–2XL. Buy
them together for the classic button-up pajama look, or wear the top with the jersey pants if you'd
rather have the warmth. Note that the women's set is made by our print partner in China and ships
from there, so it takes the longest to arrive (see the dates below).

**The hoodie** is for the family member who won't wear pajamas in a photo. The
[Christmas Dinosaur Pattern Hoodie](/products/christmas-dinosaur-pattern-hoodie) is the same pattern
on a unisex hoodie with a brushed fleece inside and a double-lined hood, 2XS–6XL. It's the one piece
here that works just as well at a Christmas party on the 23rd as it does on the couch on the 25th.

## For the kids: matching, but not sleepwear

This is the part we want to be clear about. The kids' and baby pieces match the print exactly, but
**they are not children's sleepwear.** Their listings say so: they're made for everyday wear and
play as part of a matching family outfit. So treat them as the kids' half of the Christmas outfit,
for the photo, Christmas morning, the drive to grandma's or the school concert, and put the kids to
bed in their usual pajamas.

- **[Kids' leggings](/products/cozy-christmas-dinos-kids-christmas-dinosaur-leggings), 2T–7, $34.99.**
  Soft stretch microfiber with an elastic waistband that pulls on easily. The chart lists height
  first, from 36¼″ at 2T to 50⅜″ at 7, and the listing says to size by height, then check waist and
  hips.
- **[Youth leggings](/products/cozy-christmas-dinos-youth-christmas-dinosaur-leggings), 8–20, $34.99.**
  Four-way stretch, so they move with a big kid and keep their shape. Height runs from 52¾″ at
  size 8 to 66⅞″ at 20.
- **[Baby bodysuit](/products/little-dino-merry-christmas-baby-dinosaur-christmas-bodysuit), 12M–24M,
  $24.99.** 100% combed cotton with lap shoulders and three snaps, in White, Ash, Black or Vintage
  Indigo. It's sized by garment length, 12¾″ at 12M up to 14¾″ at 24M, so lay one of your baby's
  current bodysuits flat and compare.

The leggings are bottoms only. For a top, the evergreen print pairs well with a plain red, white or
green tee, or with one of the 14 pieces in our [kids' dinosaur hoodie collection](/collections/kids-dinosaur-hoodie).

## Questions people ask

**Do you make a kids' dinosaur Christmas pajama set?**
Not as sleepwear. The kids' and youth leggings and the baby bodysuit match the family print, but
they're made for daytime wear and play, not for sleeping in.

**Is there a men's version?**
The adult pajama pants and the hoodie are unisex, 2XS–6XL. The satin top and pants are cut for women.

**How do the adult sizes run?**
Go by the chart, not your usual letter. The pants are sized by waist and hips, and the drawstring
gives some room at the waist, so if you're between sizes the hips are the measurement to trust.

**Will the print fade?**
The leggings' listing says the colors are made to stay bright wash after wash. The adult pajama pants and
the women's top and pants are printed before they're cut and sewn, so the pattern runs right to the seams.

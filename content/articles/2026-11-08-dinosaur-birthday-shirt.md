## Getting the size right

Toddler sizes go by measurement, not by age. Plenty of two-year-olds wear 3T, and a tall three-year-old may want 4T.
The easy way to check is to lay a T-shirt that fits them now flat on a table and measure it.

| Size | Body length | Width (armpit to armpit) |
|---|---|---|
| 2T | 15½" | 12" |
| 3T | 16½" | 13" |
| 4T | 17½" | 14" |
| 5T | 18½" | 15" |

These are from the Two Rex, Four Rex and Birthday Rex listings. The tees have a relaxed fit and are pre-shrunk, so they
won't shrink much in the wash. If they're between sizes, go up: the shirt gets worn after the party too, and kids grow
fast at this age.

Name-Saurus runs in youth sizes: S is 20⅞" long and 15¼" wide, up to XL at 24⅜" × 18¼".

## When to order a dinosaur birthday shirt

Every shirt is printed when you order it, not pulled off a shelf. That's how we avoid making piles of stock that never
sells, but it means you need to allow time.

Most of the birthday listings estimate **2–5 business days to print, then 3–4 business days to ship** (US standard
shipping). That's up to nine business days, or nearly two weeks with weekends. The Three Rex listing quotes a little
longer, up to eight business days in transit.

**Our advice: order three weeks before the party.** That covers a slow week and leaves time to fix anything that goes
wrong. If it's a personalized shirt, give yourself the margin. A typo can't be swapped out the day before.

already in 3T. When in doubt, size up.

**Can I return a personalized birthday shirt?**
Personalized shirts can't be returned or exchanged for spelling or size changes. A shirt that arrives damaged or
misprinted is always replaced.

**How long does a dinosaur birthday shirt take to arrive?**
Each one is printed to order. Most listings estimate 2–5 business days to print and 3–4 to ship within the US. Order
about three weeks ahead to be safe.

**Will the shirt shrink?**
The fabric is pre-shrunk, so it's made to hold its size through washing.

you want a white or clear backing.

## Two kinds of dinosaur sticker

Both ranges are vinyl and both are printed when you order. They're made differently, though, and
the listings promise different things.

### The original range: waterproof, with a white or clear backing

Eighteen designs. The listings describe them as **premium waterproof vinyl for indoor and outdoor
use**, with a scratch-resistant surface. They're printed with eco-solvent inks over a white or
transparent underlay, and the material won't rip if you peel it back to straighten it.

Most designs let you pick the backing:

- **White** gives the dinosaur a white border, like a classic die-cut sticker. It reads well on
  any color, including a dark laptop.
- **Transparent** drops the background, so the dinosaur looks painted onto the surface. Best on
  light-colored, smooth things like a white bottle or a window.

Sizes run **2″, 3″, 4″ and 6″ square**, from $3.99 to $5.99 on most designs. Not every design
comes in every size or both backings, so check the listing.

## Which size dinosaur sticker for which surface?

Size is the decision people get wrong most often. Here's a rough guide:

- **2″: phones, planners, a kid's notebook.** Small enough for a phone case or a page corner.
  Original range only.
- **3″: the all-rounder.** Fits a water bottle, a lunch box or a laptop with room for others.
  This is the size most people mean when they say "a sticker."
- **4″: the laptop centerpiece.** One sticker that is clearly the main one. Good on a
  guitar case or a toolbox too.
- **5.5″ or 6″: a car window, a cooler, a door.** Readable from across a room or a parking lot.
- **15″ × 3.75″: a bumper or a long flat edge.** Three designs only.

**What we don't make:** wall decals, puffy or foam stickers, glow-in-the-dark stickers or sticker
sheets. If you're looking for dinosaur stickers for a bedroom wall, ours top out at 6 inches, so
they work as accents on a door or a toy box rather than a mural.

## Dinosaur stickers FAQ

**Are your dinosaur stickers waterproof?**
The original range is listed as waterproof vinyl for indoor and outdoor use. The opaque art range
is durable vinyl, but its listings don't say waterproof.

**What sizes do the stickers come in?**
From 2″ to 6″ square, plus a 15″ × 3.75″ strip on three designs. Available sizes vary by design.

**What's the difference between white and transparent?**
White gives the sticker a white background and border. Transparent is clear, so only the artwork
shows. It's offered on most original-range designs.

**Can I put one on a car?**
Yes. For a window, a 5.5″ or 6″ works well. For a bumper, look at the 15″ × 3.75″ strip.

**Do you ship outside the US?**
No. The listings say US only.

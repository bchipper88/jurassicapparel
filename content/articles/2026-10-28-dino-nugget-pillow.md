## What our dino nugget pillow actually is

Search for a dino nugget pillow and most of what comes up are fuzzy plush toys, often sold in
packs of three or five. Ours is a different thing, and it's worth knowing which one you want
before you buy.

- **It's a printed, shaped pillow.** The nugget is printed on the front of a 100% polyester
  pillow and the pillow is cut to the nugget's outline. It's soft and squashy, but the texture
  is smooth fabric, not fur.
- **One nugget, one shape.** It's the long-neck dinosaur, the one with the sweeping neck and
  tail. It's sold as one pillow, not a set.
- **3″ to 5″ thick.** A cuddle and couch pillow, not something to sleep on.
- **Printed on the front only**, with a plain white back. Face the nugget out.
- **Sealed, with no zipper.** There's no cover to remove and nothing to unzip.
- **Printed to order** on a blank sourced in the USA. It ships to US addresses only.

If what you want is a fluffy plush toy for a toddler to drag around by the tail, a plush is the
better buy. If you want the nugget itself, with its crumb and its golden edges, sitting on a
sofa, a dorm bed or an office chair, this is the one.

The listing gives each size as a square: 10″ × 10″, 16″ × 16″ and 22″ × 22″. In the listing
photos the nugget runs corner to corner, so read those numbers as the size of the square it's cut
from, not the length of the nugget.

| Size | Price | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Small, 10″ × 10″ | $29.99 | A desk, a car seat, a backpack, a toddler's hands |
| Medium, 16″ × 16″ | $31.99 | A kid's bed or an armchair; the safe gift pick |
| Large, 22″ × 22″ | $33.99 | A sofa, a full-size bed, a teen or adult who wants the joke big |

The whole range is four dollars apart, so choose on where it's going to live, not on price. If
you can't decide, the medium is right for nearly everyone. The large is the one that gets a laugh
when it comes out of the box.

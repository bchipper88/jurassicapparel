## Retro dinosaur shirt FAQ

**Are these real vintage shirts?**
No. They're new shirts printed to order in retro-style designs. If you want a genuine 1990s
museum-shop tee, you're looking at resale sites. If you want the look in your size, new, that's us.

**Which one feels most like an old, worn-in tee?**
Neon T-Rex, for kids. Its open-end cotton is the one the listing describes as having a vintage feel.
For adults, Retro Rex is a light, soft combed-cotton tee rather than a heavy one.

**Do they run true to size?**
Not all of them. Retro Rex runs big. Neon T-Rex runs small, and the polo runs small. The tank and
the skeleton tee give measurements in their size charts. The sunset tees are relaxed fits.

**What's the biggest size?**
3XL, in the Mamasaurus Sunset tee and the 90's Dinos polo. Retro Rex stops at 2XL.

**Is there a long-sleeve version?**
Not right now. Every retro design here is short-sleeved; for warmth, go for one of the retro hoodies.

Can't wait to celebrate with you." The pun is the headline. Don't stack three in a row.

## Puns hidden in real dinosaur names

The best dinosaur puns get the science right. Each of these real names has a meaning, and the
meaning has a joke in it.

| Dinosaur | What the name actually means | The pun |
|---|---|---|
| Tyrannosaurus rex | "Tyrant lizard king" | *T. wrecks*: for any toddler, any kitchen |
| Triceratops | "Three-horned face" | *Tri, tri again* |
| Brontosaurus | "Thunder lizard" | *Bronto-snore-us*: for someone who snores like thunder |
| Pachycephalosaurus | "Thick-headed lizard" | For someone stubborn. Its name already did the work |
| Ankylosaurus | "Fused lizard" | *Thank-ylosaurus*: for a thank-you card |
| Iguanodon | "Iguana tooth" | *I-guana-know*: for when you don't |
| Stegosaurus | "Roofed lizard" | *Steg-o-saw-us coming* |
| Diplodocus | "Double beam" | *Diplo-doc-us*: for a doctor, or a vet |
| Spinosaurus | "Spine lizard" | *Spin-o-saurus*: for a DJ or a cyclist |
| Allosaurus | "Different lizard" | *'Allo-saurus*: a greeting |
| Velociraptor | "Swift thief" | *Veloci-rapper*: fast talker |

Children's Museum of Indianapolis helped choose the name. Most paleontologists now think it was
a young *Pachycephalosaurus*, so the thick-headed lizard gets the last word.

## How to make your own dinosaur pun

Most dino puns come from five moves. Once you know them, you can write one for any card in
about a minute.

1. **Add -saurus.** Any noun becomes a dinosaur: *Coffee-saurus*, *Teacher-saurus*,
   *Bride-o-saurus*. It nearly always works.
2. **Swap in "rex".** It sounds like *wrecks*, *ex*, *tricks* or *-rex-tra*. *Tea-Rex* and
   *T. wrecks* both come from this.
3. **Use "dino" for "dyna" or "di".** *Dino-mite*, *dino-sore*, *dino-snore*.
4. **Borrow the era names.** *Jurassic*, *Cretaceous*, *Triassic* and *Mesozoic* all sound
   grand enough to replace "old" or "ancient".
5. **Lean on the tiny arms.** Anything the T. rex can't reach, lift or clap is a joke: hugs,
   push-ups, high fives, the top of the Christmas tree.

The best ones use two moves at once. *Tea-Rex Time* uses a swap and a sound-alike, and the
Tree Rex sweater swaps in "rex" and adds the tiny arms.

## Dinosaur puns FAQ

**What is the most famous dinosaur pun?**
"Do-you-think-he-saurus?" is the classic. It's the punchline to "What do you call a
blind dinosaur?" *Thesaurus* and *Tea-Rex* are close behind.

**What's a good dinosaur pun for a birthday?**
"Have a *roar*-some birthday" suits any age. For someone turning 40 or older, "You're not old,
you're *Jurassic*" gets the bigger laugh.

**What's a cute dinosaur pun for a Valentine?**
"You make my heart go *rawr*" is the classic. "You're the *Tri* to my *ceratops*" is for
couples who like a groan.

**Are dinosaur puns and dinosaur jokes the same thing?**
Not quite. A pun is wordplay, like *dino-mite*. A joke has a setup and a punchline: "Why can't
a T. rex clap? Because they're extinct." This page sticks to puns.

**Is a pterodactyl a dinosaur?**
No. Pterodactyls were pterosaurs, flying reptiles that were close cousins of the dinosaurs. The
puns still count.

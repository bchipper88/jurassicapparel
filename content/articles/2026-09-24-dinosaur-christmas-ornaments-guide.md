# Dinosaur Ornaments for Your Christmas Tree: Every Design, Shape and Order-By Date

Somebody in your house has decided the tree needs a T-Rex this year. Good. The question is
which one — and whether it will arrive before the tree goes up. This guide walks through every
dinosaur ornament we make: what they're made of, the shapes and designs, what they cost, the
mugs and wrapping paper that match them, and the latest date to order for Christmas.

## What our wooden dinosaur ornaments are like

Most of what we sell in this category is a wooden ornament, and it's worth knowing what that
means before you picture a glass bauble.

They're cut from **solid Hinoki wood** — a light, pale cypress — and they're **9 mm thick
(0.37″)**, so they feel substantial in the hand without dragging a branch down. The design is
**printed on both sides**, which matters more than it sounds: an ornament turns on its ribbon,
and a blank back is what you'd otherwise see half the time. Each one comes with a **red paper
ribbon** threaded through a hole at the top.

The detail people don't expect: they have a **magnetic back**. When the tree comes down, the
T-Rex can go on the fridge instead of into a box in the attic.

### The six shapes, and how big they are

Every wooden design is sold in all six shapes, so pick the design first and the shape second.

| Shape | Length | Width | Good for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bell | 4⅛″ | 3⅜″ | A classic silhouette on a busy tree |
| Heart | 4¾″ | 3½″ | A gift for a partner or a first Christmas |
| Oval | 5″ | 3⅜″ | Showing off the most of a pattern |
| Star | 4″ | 3⅜″ | Smaller branches, or near the top |
| Stocking | 5⅛″ | 4″ | The largest; hangs well on a mantel garland too |
| Tree | 5¼″ | 3½″ | The tallest; a tree on a tree, which kids find hilarious |

At four to five inches, these are larger than a standard ball ornament. On a smaller tabletop
tree, one or two will be plenty.

## The eight wooden dinosaur ornament designs

Each design is an all-over pattern, so whichever shape you choose, the dinosaurs fill it edge to
edge.

- **Gingerbread Skeleton** — gingerbread-cookie dinosaur skeletons, iced in white, some
  wearing Santa hats and holly, on a white background with little brown stars. The one for
  people who like their dinosaurs as fossils.
- **Gingerbread Dinosaurs** — whole gingerbread dinos, Christmas trees and stockings scattered
  over a deep navy background. The darkest of the set, and it stands out against green branches.
- **Pink Christmas** — soft green dinosaurs, palm trees and presents on pale pink. The pick for
  a pink-and-pastel tree.
- **Jolly Dinosaurs** — green dinos in red sweaters and Santa hats on a cream background, in a
  loose, sketched style.
- **Snow Globe Dinosaur** — little dinosaurs inside snow globes, among Christmas trees and
  sparkles, on a warm cream background.
- **Dino Holiday Cheer** — watercolor-style stegosaurs and T-Rexes in Santa hats and sweaters, with
  bunting and candy canes, on white. The gentlest look of the eight.
- **Festive Dino Wonderland** — the busiest pattern: red, green and blue dinosaurs with candy
  canes, presents and Santa in his sleigh, on cream. The most traditionally "Christmas" colours.
- **Tree Rex** — T-Rexes with the words *TREE REX* in bold green over red-and-green plaid. The
  loudest of the set and the only one with lettering.

**If you're buying one for a T-Rex fan specifically**, *Tree Rex* is the one built around it;
*Jolly Dinosaurs*, *Festive Dino Wonderland* and *Dino Holiday Cheer* all include T-Rexes among
a mix of species.

## The ceramic option: Watercolor Dino

The ninth ornament is a different product. *Watercolor Dino* is **white ceramic** with a
**golden string**, printed on **one side**, in a soft watercolor style. It comes in three
shapes — circle (2.84″ across), heart and oval — and three designs: *Butterfly Dino*, *Pink
Princess Dino* and *Blue Prince Dino*.

It's noticeably smaller than the wooden ornaments, at under 3½″ in every shape, and the baby
dinosaur artwork makes it the natural choice for a young child's first ornament. Ceramic is
more fragile than wood, so hang it a little higher if there's a toddler or a cat around.

## When to order dinosaur ornaments for Christmas

Everything here is printed to order rather than pulled off a shelf, so the date you order
matters. Each listing gives its own production and shipping estimates for standard US shipping:

| Item | Production | Shipping | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wooden ornaments | 1–4 business days | 3–6 business days | 4–10 business days |
| Wrapping paper | 2–3 business days | 3–6 business days | 5–9 business days |
| Mugs | 1–2 business days | 2–5 business days | 3–7 business days |

Tracking is sent once an order moves from production into shipping, so a few quiet days after
you order are normal.

Working back from the slow end of those estimates:

- **To have wooden ornaments on the tree for Thanksgiving weekend** (November 27–29), order by
  about **November 11**.
- **For Christmas, order the wooden ornaments by about December 9.** Christmas Day falls on a
  Friday this year, and the slowest estimate puts a December 9 order at your door on the 23rd.
- **Ordering a set?** Go by the slowest item, which is the ornament. And remember the wrapping
  paper has to arrive *before* you can wrap anything.

Those dates are arithmetic on the store's own estimates, not a carrier guarantee, and December
is when carriers run late. The first week of December is the comfortable window; earlier is
better.

**Outside the US?** The wooden ornaments and the wrapping paper ship to the US, Canada, Europe,
the UK, Australia and New Zealand only. The listings give delivery estimates for the US only,
so allow extra time elsewhere.

## Dinosaur ornament FAQ

**What are the dinosaur ornaments made of?**
Eight of our nine are solid Hinoki wood, 9 mm thick, printed on both sides, with a red paper
ribbon and a magnetic back. The ninth, *Watercolor Dino*, is white ceramic with a golden
string, printed on one side.

**Are the wooden ornaments printed on both sides?**
Yes. The ceramic *Watercolor Dino* is printed on the front only.

**How big are they?**
The wooden shapes run from 4″ (star) to 5¼″ (tree) in length and 3⅜″ to 4″ wide. The ceramic
ornament is under 3½″ in every shape.

**Can I use them as fridge magnets?**
The wooden ones, yes — they have a magnetic back, so they work on the fridge once the tree
comes down.

**When should I order for Christmas?**
For the wooden ornaments, by about December 9 based on the listings' slowest estimates, and
ideally in the first week of December. By about November 11 if you want them on the tree for
Thanksgiving weekend.

**Do you ship ornaments internationally?**
The wooden ornaments ship to the US, Canada, Europe, the UK, Australia and New Zealand. The
listed delivery estimates are for the US.

**Can I get the same design on a mug or wrapping paper?**
Yes. Six designs have both a matching mug and matching wrapping paper; *Tree Rex* and *Festive
Dino Wonderland* have a matching mug.

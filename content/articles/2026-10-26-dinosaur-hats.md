# Dinosaur Hats: Bucket Hats, Dad Hats, Beanies and How to Pick One

A dinosaur hat is the easiest way to wear the obsession without committing a whole outfit to it. It goes on
for the school run, comes off for the meeting, and works as a gift for someone whose size you only half know.

embroidered trucker cap and three winter beanies. Here is what each one is like to wear, who it suits, and
when to order if it's going under a tree.

## Dinosaur bucket hats: the loud ones

The bucket hats are where the prints get to be big. Every one is reversible, so you're really buying two hats,
a printed side and a plain one, in a soft polyester with a linen-like feel that wicks sweat. The designs run
from busy to busier: the jungle scene on the [Dino Jungle bucket hat](/products/dino-jungle-dinosaur-bucket-hat),
a Retro 90's pattern, Neon Dinos, Colorful Dino's, a Prehistoric Fossils print, Dinosaur Vacation, Dino Dads,
the Dino Nuggies print, and a 4th of July Party design that is clearly a summer hat.

**Sizing is the one thing to get right.** These come in three sizes, measured around the top of the hat:

- **XS** — 21⅞″
- **S/M** — 23⅜″
- **L/XL** — 25″

Run a soft tape measure around the head just above the ears and pick the size at or a little above that
number. A bucket hat that's too small rides up; one that's slightly big just sits lower, which is the look
anyway.

Honest note on season: bucket hats are a sunshine item. They make a good gift now if the person travels or
lives somewhere warm, but if you're shopping for a cold December, look at the beanies first.

## Dinosaur dad hats: the safe gift

If you don't know someone's head size, buy a dad hat. They're one size with an adjustable strap and an antique
buckle (20½″ to 24⅜″ around), unstructured and low-profile, in soft chino cotton twill with six stitched
ventilation eyelets. Most come in five or six colors, from black and navy to stone, pink, light blue and white.

The designs are quieter than the bucket hats, which is the point. Three small-mark designs (Brontosaurus Mini,
T-Rex Mini, Triceratops Mini) suit people who want the dinosaur to be a nod rather than a shout. The T-Rex
Skeleton and Styracosaurus dad hats are the two at $20.00, the lowest price in the range. The Raptor Skeleton
sits at $29.99.

The one with the most personality is the [embroidered Dino Nuggies dad hat](/products/dino-nuggies-embroidered-dinosaur-dad-hat):
a golden T-rex-shaped chicken nugget stitched on the front panel. If the person you're buying for already has
the shirt, this is the matching piece. Our [dino nuggets shirt guide](/blogs/blog/dino-nuggets-shirt) covers
the rest of that range.

## The dinosaur trucker hat

There's one, and it's a different animal from the dad hats. The
[T-Rex embroidered trucker hat](/products/t-rex-embroidered-dinosaur-trucker-hat) is a structured six-panel cap
with a hard front panel, a breathable mesh back and a Permacurv visor, so it holds its shape rather than
slouching. The roaring T-rex head is embroidered in black, white and grey. It comes in six colorways, including
two-tone black/white, navy/white and charcoal/black, with a plastic adjustable closure at the back.

Pick the trucker over a dad hat if the wearer likes a cap that stands up on its own, or spends warm days
outside and wants the airflow.

## Which dinosaur hat should you buy?

- **For a gift when you don't know their size:** a dad hat. Adjustable, inoffensive, fits most adults.
- **For a child:** the Dino Nuggies beanie (the listing says it fits kids), or a bucket hat in XS once you've
  measured.
- **For someone who'll actually wear it every day:** a Mini dad hat in a neutral color.
- **For winter, right now:** a beanie. Fossil Hunter for the grown-up paleontologist, Museum Dinosaurs for the
  person with the matching pajamas.
- **For a vacation or a festival:** a bucket hat. Reversible, so it packs as two.
- **On a budget:** the T-Rex Skeleton or Styracosaurus dad hat at $20.00.

## FAQ

**Do dinosaur hats come in kids' sizes?**
The bucket hats come in XS (21⅞″ around), and the Dino Nuggies beanie is listed as fitting kids and adults. The
dad and trucker hats are one adjustable adult size. Measure first.

**Are the designs embroidered or printed?**
It depends on the hat. The trucker hat, the Dino Nuggies dad hat and the Fossil Hunter and Dino Nuggies beanies
are embroidered. The bucket hats and the Museum Dinosaurs beanie are printed.

**How do I wash them?**
The Fossil Hunter and Dino Nuggies knit beanies are listed as hand washable. The Museum Dinosaurs beanie's
listing says to wash at 86°F (30°C). The cap listings don't give care instructions.

**Do you ship outside the US?**
The hat listings show standard shipping for the US only, and the estimates above are for that.

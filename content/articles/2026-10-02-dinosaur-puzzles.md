# Dinosaur Puzzles: 120, 252 or 520 Pieces, and Which One to Give

A dinosaur puzzle is one of the few gifts that works for a nine-year-old and their
grandparent at the same table. The trick is picking the right piece count. Too few and it's
done before the cocoa cools; too many and it lives in the box until March.

We make 13 dinosaur jigsaw puzzles in two styles. Below is what each one is, how big it ends up,
who it suits, and the dates to order by for Christmas. Every size, price and shipping time here
comes from the live product listing.

## Which dinosaur puzzle for which person?

Piece count is the whole decision, so here's how the three play out:

- **120 pieces: a younger puzzler or a short sit.** A good step up for a school-age kid who has
  outgrown chunky toddler puzzles. Not for children under 3; the flat puzzle listings carry a
  small-parts warning, and that's sensible for any jigsaw.
- **252 pieces: the family puzzle.** Big enough to share across a kitchen table, small enough to
  finish in an evening or a weekend afternoon. At $24.99, the flat 252 is the best-value puzzle
  in the range. The jar 252 is $39.99 and a little larger.
- **520 pieces: for adults.** Nearly 20 inches across. The flat designs suit a grown-up
  who'd rather frame the result than tip it back in the box. If you're shopping for dinosaur
  puzzles for adults, start here.

**What we don't make:** wooden puzzles, floor puzzles, or chunky-piece puzzles for toddlers.
Ours are proper jigsaws, from 120 pieces up.

## Ordering a dinosaur puzzle for Christmas

Each puzzle is printed when you order it, and the two styles have very different lead times:

- **Flat puzzles:** 1–3 business days to make, then 2–5 to ship. Up to 8 business days.

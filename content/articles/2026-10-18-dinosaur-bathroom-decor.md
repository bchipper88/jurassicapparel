# Dinosaur Bathroom Decor: What Survives the Steam, What to Buy First, and Two Rooms Priced Out

A bathroom is the easiest room in the house to give a dinosaur theme. It's small, so a few pieces fill
it. But it's also the hardest room on decor. Steam curls paper, wet feet soak the floor, and anything
near the tub gets splashed. So good dinosaur bathroom decor is decor that copes with water. Whether it
has a dinosaur on it is the easy part.

This guide goes surface by surface: floor, walls, tiles, towels, windowsill. It says what holds up and
what doesn't, then prices out a kids' bathroom and a grown-up one. Everything named is a real listing
in our shop today, with the price and size from that listing. One thing first: we don't sell shower
curtains, hand towels or soap dispensers. Those are below too, as gaps to fill elsewhere.

## Walls: posters, and how to keep them flat in a steamy room

A wall is where a dinosaur bathroom gets its personality, and it's where most of them go wrong. Our
posters are printed on thick matte paper (10.3 mil, 189 g/m²). That's good paper, but it's still
paper. Hung bare in a bathroom where people take long hot showers, any paper print will eventually
ripple.

Three ways to keep it flat:

1. **Buy it framed.** The [Fossil Hunter framed poster](/products/fossil-hunter-framed-dinosaur-poster)
   ($49.99) comes in a ¾″ ayous wood frame with an acrylic front and hanging hardware. The design is a
   vintage dig-site badge: a T-rex skull, a pickaxe and brush, and "Fossil Hunter · Dig Crew"
   lettering. It's the safest wall piece we sell for a bathroom.
2. **Frame an unframed print yourself.** Any frame with a glazed front protects the paper. Hang it on
   the wall farthest from the shower.
3. **Fix the steam, not the poster.** Run the fan or crack the window for 15 minutes after a shower.
   That protects the paint and grout as well as the print.

Which print? It depends on who uses the bathroom:

- **For kids:** the [Dinosaur Chart with Names poster](/products/dinosaur-chart-with-names-dinosaur-poster)
  shows 12 dinosaurs, each labeled with its name, its period, and whether it ate plants or meat. It
  gives a child something to read while brushing their teeth. It's $24.99 for 12″ × 16″, up to $34.99
  for 24″ × 32″.
- **For adults:** the [Velociraptor Natural History Plate](/products/velociraptor-natural-history-plate-dinosaur-poster)
  is a feathered *Velociraptor mongoliensis* in the style of an old scientific illustration. It looks
  like a print from a natural history museum, not a kids' room. It's $15.99 for 8″ × 10″, and 11″ × 14″

## FAQ

**Will a paper poster survive in a bathroom?**
In a well-ventilated bathroom, framed and away from the shower, yes. Unframed paper in a steamy room
with no fan will ripple over time, whatever brand it is. If steam is a problem, the framed Fossil
Hunter poster with its acrylic front is the safer choice.

**Can I put dinosaur stickers on bathroom tiles?**
Yes. Our stickers are waterproof vinyl rated for indoor and outdoor use. Put them on clean, dry, smooth
tile, and keep them out of the direct shower spray.

**What size bath mat do I need?**
For in front of a standard 60″ tub, the 34″ × 21″. For in front of a sink or in a small powder room,
the 24″ × 17″.

**Is dinosaur bathroom decor only for kids?**
No. Pick the artwork that suits the adults: a natural-history plate, the museum-style bath mat, a
succulent. That reads as a collector's bathroom, not a nursery. For a kids' room, go brighter with
the labeled chart and the bubble-bath sticker.

Not at the moment. See "What we don't sell" above.

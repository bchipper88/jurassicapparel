## Duvet cover vs. comforter vs. bed set

A lot of people searching for dinosaur bedding have a **dinosaur comforter** in mind: one quilted
piece, print and all. What we make is a duvet cover, and for a kid's bed it's usually the better buy.

- **A comforter** is the filling and the print sewn together. When it gets juice on it (it will), the
  whole thing goes in the machine, and a twin comforter is a tight fit in many home washers. When your
  kid outgrows the print, you replace all of it.
- **A duvet cover** is just the printed case. The plain insert stays on the bed for years; the cover
  comes off and washes like a big pillowcase. Change the theme later and you're buying a cover, not a
  whole new comforter.
- **A bed set** bundles a comforter or cover with sheets and pillowcases. We don't sell sets. Our
  covers are sold on their own, so pair one with plain sheets in a color pulled from the print.

If the bed already has a plain comforter, you can often zip that straight inside, which makes the
cover the cheapest way to turn an ordinary bed into a dinosaur bed.

Duvet sizes and mattress sizes aren't the same numbers, which is where most returns come from. Here

The listings allow for up to 3″ either way on every size.

Three things that table settles:

- **There is no Full size, and you don't need one.** Our Queen is listed as fitting Full and Queen.
  On a Full it hangs a little longer at the sides, which on a kid's bed pushed against a wall is no
  bad thing.
- **Twin XL is for dorm beds and some bunk beds.** It's the same width as the Twin but 4″ longer. If
  you're not sure, measure the mattress length: 75″ is a Twin, 80″ is a Twin XL.
- **Toddler beds and cribs** aren't on the list. A Twin cover will swamp a toddler mattress, so for
  now we'd keep it for the move to a big-kid bed.

## What insert to put inside

The cover needs something to cover. Two rules:

1. **Match the size.** A Twin cover wants a twin insert; a Queen cover wants a full/queen insert. Look
   for an insert listed at roughly the cover's measurements. One an inch or two smaller slides in
   more easily than one that's oversized.
2. **Pick the weight for the room, not the season.** The cover itself is lightweight microfiber, so
   all the warmth comes from the insert. A kid who runs hot does better with a light insert and a
   throw at the foot of the bed for cold nights.

There are no corner ties listed on our covers, so if the insert shifts, shake it into the corners
before you zip it shut. Doing it with the cover inside out, then flipping it over the insert, is the
fastest way we know.

## Washing and living with it

From the care label: machine wash cold, no hotter than 90°F (30°C), and don't bleach, iron, dry clean
or tumble dry with heat. In practice that means a cold wash and a low or no-heat dry, or a line. Zip
it closed before it goes in the machine so the zipper doesn't catch on anything else.

The back is plain white. Fold the top third of the duvet down and you get a crisp white band across
the bed, which quiets a busy print and looks deliberately made.

## Dinosaur bedding FAQ

**Is this a dinosaur comforter?**
No. It's a duvet cover, the printed case that goes over a comforter or duvet insert. Put a plain
comforter inside and it works the same way, with the bonus that the cover comes off to wash.

**Do you sell a dinosaur bed set with sheets and pillowcases?**
No. Each cover is sold on its own, and pillowcases, sheets and inserts aren't included. Plain sheets
in one of the print's colors look better than a second dinosaur pattern anyway.

**What size do I need for a full bed?**
The Queen. It's listed as fitting Full and Queen and hangs about 17″ past each side of a full
mattress.

**Is it warm enough for winter?**
The cover is lightweight microfiber, so warmth depends on the insert you put inside. Add a throw for
cold rooms. We make a dozen in our [dinosaur blankets](/collections/dinosaur-blankets) collection.

**Will it fit a toddler bed or crib?**
No. The smallest size is a Twin (67″×88″), which is too big for a toddler mattress.

**Is the print on both sides?**
The print is on the front. The back is white.

**Which one is best for a boy and a girl sharing a room?**
Colorful Dinosaurs. It mixes every color on warm cream, so neither bed reads as "the boy's" or "the
girl's", and the two beds match.

## Women's relaxed tee: the ladies' dinosaur shirt most people mean

This is the cut with a women's pattern: a crew neck, side seams and a relaxed body that skims
rather than clings. The cotton is combed and ring-spun, which is what gives it the soft, smooth
hand, and it's pre-shrunk. Solid colors are 100% cotton; the heather colors are blends (most are
52% cotton, 48% polyester), so pick a heather if you like a drapier, slightly stretchier tee.

Sizes run S to 3XL. The listings don't print a size chart for this cut, so if you're between
sizes, size from a relaxed-fit tee you already own rather than a fitted one.

The designs on this cut are mostly for mothers:

- **Mamasaurus Sunset:** a T. rex silhouette against a striped retro sunset, with one small rex
  riding on its back and another trotting alongside. The classic "mom of two" mamasaurus.
- **Realistic Piggyback Mamasaurus:** a painted, realistic T. rex carrying a baby on its back.
  For someone who'd rather wear the museum than the cartoon.
- **Simple Brontosaurus Mamasaurus:** a single line-drawn long-neck on a horizon line. The
  quietest design in the range.
- **[Don't Mess With Mamasaurus](/products/dont-mess-with-mamasaurus-womens-dinosaur-t-shirt):**
  a roaring blue rex under "or you'll get Jurasskicked." The loud one.
- **Dinosaur Mom:** the word MOM in white block letters, with dinosaurs cut out of each letter.
- **Mommysaurus:** a triceratops and baby in white line art with a small red heart.
- **[Coming Soon](/products/coming-soon-womens-dinosaur-shirt):** a baby dinosaur curled inside a
  pastel egg. It's a pregnancy announcement you can wear, and it photographs well.

There's one that isn't about motherhood at all:
**[Mandala Dubreuillosaurus](/products/mandala-dubreuillosaurus-womens-dinosaur-shirt)**, a
dinosaur in a circle of jungle ferns under a patterned sunset, drawn in a dense mosaic style. It's
the one to buy for a woman who simply likes dinosaurs.

The full set of mom designs, plus matching hoodies and mugs, is in
[the Mamasaurus collection](/collections/mamasaurus).

## The unisex tee: more sizes, straighter cut

A lot of our dinosaur shirts for women are cut unisex: the same 4.2 oz combed cotton, but a
straight, unisex body. If you prefer a boxier fit, want to knot the hem, or need a
size outside S–3XL, this is the cut to look at. Most designs run XS to 4XL, and the unisex
listings include a size chart; on the adult shirt chart a medium is 29 inches long and 20 inches
across the chest, laid flat.

Examples in the women's collection include the Japanese-print T. rex designs (Geisha, Samurai),
Pteroble Puns, Winosaur, and
**[Jurassic Flowery Night](/products/jurassic-flowery-night-womens-dinosaur-shirt)**, a white
T. rex and raptor in a bed of pink roses under a crescent moon. That last one comes in XS–4XL and
eight colors including Heather Prism Lilac and Pink.

The unisex designs sit alongside the men's in our
[adult dinosaur shirt collection](/collections/adult-dinosaur-shirt), which is where to browse if
you're buying matching shirts for a couple.

## Shipping, and when to order for Christmas

Every shirt is printed when you order, which is why none ship the same day. Each listing gives two
phases: production, then shipping, US only, with a tracking number sent once it ships. The cuts
differ:

- **Women's relaxed tee:** 2–4 business days, then 3–8 in transit. Order by **December 3** for
  Christmas.
- **Unisex tee:** 2–6, then 2–5. Order by **December 4**.
- **Tank top:** 2–4, then 7–10. Order by **December 1**.

Those dates allow the full maximum on both phases plus a few days for the holiday rush at the
carriers. Earlier is safer.

## Ladies' dinosaur shirt FAQ

**What's the difference between the women's tee and the unisex tee?**
The women's tee is a relaxed women's cut, S–3XL. The unisex tee is a straighter cut, usually
XS–4XL. Same cotton weight.

**Are they 100% cotton?**
The solid colors on both tees are. Heather colors are cotton-polyester blends. The tank tops are
polyester and spandex.

**Do you have plus sizes?**
The women's relaxed tee goes to 3XL and most unisex tees to 4XL. We also carry
[plus-size dinosaur leggings](/collections/dinosaur-leggings-plus-size).

**Is there a long-sleeve or warmer option?**
Not as a tee. For colder months see [our dinosaur sweatshirt guide](/blogs/blog/dinosaur-sweatshirts),
and the Mamasaurus designs also come as hoodies.

**Do you ship outside the US?**
The shirt listings state US shipping only.

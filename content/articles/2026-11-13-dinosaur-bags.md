# Dinosaur Bags: Which One to Buy, by Who It's For and When It Has to Arrive

Searching for a dinosaur bag usually means one of two things. You're buying for a child who wants
dinosaurs on everything they carry, or you're buying for a grown-up who has never really grown out
of them. Those two people need very different bags, and so does a bag that has to be under a tree
by December 25.

We make seven kinds of dinosaur bag: backpacks, purses, totes, a duffle, a fanny pack, a drawstring
bag and a laptop sleeve. This guide sorts them by who's carrying it and what goes inside, then gives
the last safe order date for each one this Christmas. Every size, price and lead time comes from the
live product listings.

## A dinosaur bag for a kid: start with their age

For children the size of the bag matters more than the print, because a bag that's too big
is uncomfortable to carry and doesn't get used.

**Ages 4 to 7: the Neon Rex backpack in Child size.** It's the only backpack we make in a smaller
size, and the only one we'd put on a kindergartner. It has padded, adjustable straps, a mesh back
panel, one main compartment and a front pocket. Our other backpacks are almost 17 inches tall, and
on a five-year-old they hang below the hips.

**Ages 8 and up: any of the 13 printed backpacks.** They're all the same full-size school bag:
water-resistant polyester, a separate sleeve for a laptop or Chromebook up to 15 inches, a zipped
front pocket and a hidden zipped pocket on the back panel that can't be opened while the bag is on.
The [T-Rex backpack](/products/t-rex-kids-dinosaur-backpack) is the obvious choice for a T-Rex fan.
Despite "Kids" in some product names, none of the 13 is smaller than the others. The
[dinosaur backpacks guide](/blogs/blog/dinosaur-backpacks) covers every print and what fits inside.

**For sports, swimming or a sleepover: the drawstring bag.** The Blue and White Raptors drawstring
bag is 15″ × 17″ polyester with a drawstring closure and twin cotton handles, rated to 33 lb. It's
light enough to live in a school bag and big enough for a towel and swimsuit or a change of clothes.

**The thing we don't make:** a toddler backpack. Nothing in the range is sized for a two- or
three-year-old, so we'd rather say so than sell you one they'll drag along the floor.

## A dinosaur bag for an adult: match it to what they carry

**Phone, wallet, keys, sunglasses: a dinosaur purse.** Most of our 24 purses are a faux leather
crossbody, 11″ × 8″ × 1.5″, with a zip top and two removable straps (a wrist strap and a shoulder
strap that adjusts from a 14″ to a 27″ drop). The [Crazy 90's print](/products/crazy-90s-womens-dinosaur-purse)
is the loudest one. The fossil and skeleton prints are the quietest, and those are the ones to
choose for work. Three prints come as a waterproof canvas saddle bag instead, and two as a
[mini clutch](/products/raindrop-dinos-dinosaur-mini-clutch) that's really a zip pouch for cards
and keys. All 24 are in [our dinosaur purses collection](/collections/dinosaur-purses), and the
[dinosaur purses guide](/blogs/blog/dinosaur-purses) compares the three styles.

**Books, lunch and a sweater: a tote bag.** Most of our totes are one 15-inch square spun-polyester
bag with cotton denim handles, rated at 2.6 gallons and up to 44 lb. A handful are a cotton blend
printed on both sides, and some of those come in 13, 16 or 18 inches. At $24.99 a tote is the
cheapest dinosaur bag we make, and it's an easy gift for a teacher or a coworker. Browse them in
[our dinosaur tote bag collection](/collections/dinosaur-tote-bag), or read the
[dinosaur tote bags guide](/blogs/blog/dinosaur-tote-bags), which lists every design and size.

**A laptop for work or campus: the full-size backpack or a laptop sleeve.** The printed backpacks
take a 15-inch laptop in their own sleeve, and the all-over prints (Colorful Fossils, Dinosaur
Skeletons, Construction Dinos) look fine on an adult commuter. If they already have a work bag they
like, the [dinosaur laptop sleeve](/products/dinosaur-laptop-bag-pink-retro-t-rex) slips inside it.
It's a snug neoprene sleeve with a faux fur lining and a top zip, in 13″ or 15″, and it has no strap
or handle, so it isn't a bag to carry on its own.

**A weekend away: the duffle bag.** The Colorful Dinos duffle is our biggest bag, 22″ × 11.5″ ×
11.5″ in heavy 9 oz polyester, with padded carry handles, a removable padded shoulder strap, a mesh
side pocket and several pockets inside. It's a gym bag, an overnight bag or a sports-kit bag. At
$99.99 it's also our most expensive, so it suits a big gift better than a small one.

**Hands free at a museum or a theme park: the fanny pack.** The Colorful Dinos fanny pack is
13″ × 6.5″ × 2¾″, holds 1.4 litres, and comes in two sizes, S/M and M/L. It's water-resistant
polyester with a two-slider top zip and a small inner pocket. Phone, keys, snacks and a ticket fit in
it, and it leaves both hands free for holding a small hand or a camera.

a tracking number when it leaves production.

## Dinosaur bag FAQ

**What's the best dinosaur bag for a 5-year-old?**
The Neon Rex backpack in Child size (ages 4 to 7). For a second bag, the drawstring bag is light
enough for a small child to carry to swimming or a sleepover.

**Is there a dinosaur bag for school?**
Yes: any of our 14 backpacks. The 13 printed ones are full-size with a 15″ laptop sleeve, which
suits ages 8 and up. The Neon Rex comes in Child, Youth and Adult sizes for younger kids.

**Which dinosaur bags are waterproof?**
The canvas saddle-bag purse is listed as waterproof. The backpacks and the fanny pack are
water-resistant, which covers rain on the way somewhere but not a soaking. The laptop sleeve is
neoprene and listed as resistant to water.

**What's the cheapest dinosaur bag?**
The tote bags, at $24.99. The drawstring bag is $27.99 and the mini clutch $29.99.

next to a Triceratops. The other has a sofa that needs a T. rex on it. Both are fair, and they
call for different pillows.

### How they're made

All six are built the same way, and it's worth knowing before you buy:

- **100% polyester**, soft and plush rather than firm.
- **3″ to 5″ thick.** That's a squashy decorative pillow, not a sleeping pillow. It won't
  replace the one under their head.
- **Printed on one side only, with a solid white back.** Face it outward on the bed.
- **Sealed, with no zipper.** There's no cover to take off and nothing for small fingers to
  unzip. The flip side is that you can't swap or wash a cover separately.
- **Printed to order** on a blank sourced in the USA, and they ship to US addresses only.

## Which size dinosaur pillow?

Size is the decision people get wrong most often, so here's how the three work in practice:

- **Small (10″ × 10″), $29.99.** About the size of a dinner plate. It's the one a toddler can
  carry from room to room, and the one that fits in a car seat or a backpack for a sleepover.
  If it's for a two- to four-year-old, start here. (We cover what else fits that age in
  [our guide to dinosaur gifts for 3 year olds](/blogs/blog/dinosaur-gifts-for-3-year-olds).)
- **Medium (16″ × 16″), $31.99.** The all-rounder. Big enough to hug, small enough to live on a
  twin bed without taking it over. For a school-age kid, this is the one.
- **Large (22″ × 22″), $33.99–$34.54.** This is a statement. On a sofa or a full-size bed it
  looks great. On a toddler bed it takes up most of the room.

The step from small to large is under $5, so pick on size, not price.

## Dinosaur pillow FAQ

**Can a child sleep on a dinosaur pillow?**
They're decorative pillows, 3–5″ thick and soft. Fine for cuddling and lounging. For a
toddler's sleeping pillow, use one made for that and keep this one as the bedtime buddy.

**Is there a T. rex pillow?**
Yes. Two of the square pillows are T. rex prints:
[Pink Retro T-Rex](/products/dinosaur-throw-pillow-pink-retro-t-rex) and
[American Pride T-Rex](/products/dinosaur-throw-pillow-american-pride-t-rex).

**Does the cover come off?**
On the square pillows, yes: there's a concealed zipper. The shaped pillows are sealed, with no
zipper and no removable cover.

**Is the back printed too?**
On the shaped pillows, no. The print is on the front and the back is plain white.

**Do you ship outside the US?**
The shaped pillows ship to US addresses only, according to their listings.

**What size is best as a gift for a kid?**
Small (10″) for toddlers, medium (16″) for school-age kids. The large is for teens, adults and
sofas.

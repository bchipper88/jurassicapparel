reindeer jumper.

## Two different sweatshirts under the prints

**The premium crewneck** (Skeleton & Pumpkins and Happy Halloween Broom). It has a 100% cotton
face over a 65/35 cotton-poly fleece, tightly knit 3-end fleece, side seams and double-stitched rib
at the collar, cuffs and hem. It's the heavier, more structured of the two. **It runs small,** and
the listing says so plainly: order one size up. Sizes S–3XL.

**The classic crewneck** (everything else for adults). A 50/50 cotton-poly blend, pre-shrunk,
classic fit, with air-jet spun yarn for a soft face and a rib collar with a little spandex in it
so the neck keeps its shape. Order your usual size. Sizes S–5XL, which makes it the one to choose
for anyone who needs above a 3XL.

Prices are $41.99 up to XL on both, then step up with size: $43.99 for 2XL and $45.99 for 3XL,
with the classic going on to $47.99 (4XL) and $49.99 (5XL). The exception is T-Rex Winter Forest,
which is $41.99 in every size.

## When to order

Every sweatshirt is printed when you order it, so the wait is make time plus shipping. All seven
listings give the same window: **2–5 business days to print, then 3–6 business days to ship**, US
only. You get a tracking code when it ships. Plan on up to 11 business days.

- **For Halloween (Saturday, October 31): order by Thursday, October 15.** That gets it to you by
  Friday the 30th even at the slow end of both windows.
- **For Christmas: order by Friday, December 4** for delivery by around December 21. Later than
  that, it may still arrive in time, but it isn't something we can count on.

keep up. Good dinosaur clothes need to survive the playground and the washing machine, fit a child
who grew an inch since summer, and still feel special enough to wrap for Christmas. This guide covers
what we make for toddlers and kids, sorted by age, with the sizes, fabrics and prices from each
listing. There's also a section on matching the grown-ups, and a table of order-by dates for the
holidays.

Everything here is printed when you order it, so nothing is sitting in a warehouse. That's why the

## Questions parents ask

**What's the smallest size?**
For this guide, 2T. Below that, the baby range starts at newborn; see the
[infant dinosaur clothes guide](/blogs/blog/infant-dinosaur-clothes). The matching Christmas baby
bodysuit runs 12M–24M.

**Are the kids' sizes boys' or girls'?**
The kids' tees and youth hoodies are a relaxed unisex fit. The "boys" and "girls" labels on our
collections are about the designs, not the cut, so shop both.

**Do the kids' hoodies have drawstrings?**
No. The Little Roar toddler hoodie and the youth hoodies in this guide are all listed without
drawstrings.

**Can the Christmas leggings be worn as pajamas?**
The listing says they're made for everyday wear and play, not as children's sleepwear. For
Christmas morning, they're for the photo and the present-opening, not for bed.

**What if my child is between sizes?**
For tees and hoodies, size up. They're pre-shrunk, but kids grow faster than cotton shrinks. For
leggings, follow the listing and choose by height.

## How to make your own dinosaur nickname

Most dinosaur nicknames are built one of five ways. Pick a formula, drop in a name or a trait, and
say it out loud. If it makes you smile, keep it. This is the whole "dinosaur nickname generator"
in one table.

| Formula | How it works | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Name + **-saurus** | "Saurus" means lizard. Add it to any first name | Leo-saurus, Ava-saurus, Grandma-saurus |
| Name + **-rex** | "Rex" means king, so it adds a little swagger | Max-Rex, Rosie Rex, Benji-Rex |
| Trait + **-osaurus** | Describe them, then turn it into a species | Snuggleosaurus, Sleepysaurus, Snackosaurus |
| Name + **-dactyl** or **-raptor** | For quick, flighty or sneaky personalities | Pip-a-dactyl, Mila-raptor, Sneakyraptor |
| **Tiny / Little / Baby** + species | Shrinks something huge into something cute | Tiny Rex, Little Trike, Baby Bronto |

Two tips. Names ending in a vowel blend into "-saurus" best (Ava-saurus flows; Jack-saurus needs the
hyphen). And short names take "-rex" better than long ones: "Max-Rex" sticks, "Alexandra-Rex" doesn't.

## Nicknames based on real dinosaur species

Some nicknames come straight from shortening the species name, and a few are used by scientists
themselves. Paleontologists really do say "Trike" for Triceratops and "Pachy" for Pachycephalosaurus.

| Species | Nickname | Why it fits |
|---|---|---|
| Tyrannosaurus rex | Rexy, T, Tiny Arms | Its arms were about a metre long on a body around 12 metres |
| Triceratops | Trike | Three horns, four syllables, one easy nickname |
| Stegosaurus | Steggy | Its brain was famously small for its size, so it suits a sweet, simple soul |
| Velociraptor | Raptor | Only turkey-sized and feathered, so it's a good name for a small, quick kid |
| Brachiosaurus | Brachy | Its front legs were longer than its back legs, so it's for someone tall |
| Ankylosaurus | Anky | Armored all over, with a club on its tail |
| Pachycephalosaurus | Pachy, Headbutt | Had a thick, domed skull |
| Parasaurolophus | Para, Trumpet | Its hollow crest may have worked like a horn |
| Spinosaurus | Spino | Spent much of its time in and around water |
| Pteranodon | Ptery | Not a dinosaur, technically, but nobody minds in a nickname |
| Diplodocus | Dippy | After a famous museum cast; see below |
| Allosaurus | Al, Big Al | After a famous fossil; see below |

## Real dinosaur fossils with nicknames

Scientists nickname important fossils too, usually after the person who found them. These are eleven
of the most famous, and each makes a good nickname with a story behind it.

- **Sue** – the T. rex at Chicago's Field Museum, found in South Dakota in 1990 and named after
  Sue Hendrickson, who discovered it.
- **Stan** – a T. rex found in South Dakota and named after amateur paleontologist Stan Sacrison. It
  sold at auction in 2020 for $31.8 million.
- **Scotty** – a T. rex from Saskatchewan, described in 2019 as one of the largest T. rex
  skeletons ever found.
- **Big Al** – an Allosaurus found in Wyoming in 1991, notable for how many injuries its bones show.
- **Dippy** – the cast of a Diplodocus skeleton that greeted visitors at London's Natural History
  Museum for over a century.
- **Sophie** – the most complete Stegosaurus skeleton known, also at London's Natural History Museum.
- **Trix** – a T. rex at the Naturalis museum in the Netherlands, named after Queen Beatrix.
- **Big John** – a Triceratops that sold at auction in Paris in 2021.
- **Apex** – a Stegosaurus that sold at Sotheby's in 2024 for $44.6 million, a record for a fossil.
- **Dakota** – an Edmontosaurus "mummy" found in North Dakota in 1999, with large patches of fossilized skin.
- **Leonardo** – a "mummified" Brachylophosaurus found in Montana with preserved skin impressions.

A child named after Sue or Stan gets a nickname and a bedtime story in one.

## FAQ

**What's a cute nickname for a dinosaur?**
Rexy, Steggy, Trike, Tiny Rex and Dino Nugget are the easiest. Short species names with a "-y" on
the end almost always sound cute.

**What do you call someone who loves dinosaurs?**
Dino fan is the everyday word. "Paleontologist" is the job. For a nickname, try Dino Kid, Fossil
Finder or Raptor.

**How do I make a dinosaur name out of my name?**
Add "-saurus" to the end, with a hyphen if it doesn't flow: Leo-saurus, Ava-saurus, Jack-saurus. For
a shorter version, add "Rex": Max-Rex, Rosie Rex.

**What's a cute dinosaur nickname for a boyfriend?**
Rawrsome, Cuddlesaurus, Snugglerex and "My Little Rex" are favorites. Tiny Arms works too if they can't
reach the top shelf.

**What are good cool dinosaur nicknames for a boy?**
Raptor, Spike, Rex, Talon, Spino and Big Al. They're short, they sound tough, and they're easy to shout
across a park.

**Is "Rexy" a real dinosaur nickname?**
Yes. "Rexy" or just "Rex" has been a fan nickname for Tyrannosaurus rex for decades.
"Rex" is Latin for "king", which is why it fits so well.

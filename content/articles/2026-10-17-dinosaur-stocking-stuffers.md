# Dinosaur Stocking Stuffers: Small Gifts Under $25, What Fits, and When to Order

A good stocking is mostly small things, and that's where dinosaur stocking stuffers get hard.
Most dinosaur merchandise is a shirt or a hoodie, which won't fit in a stocking and costs more than a
filler should. So this guide sticks to the small end of our shop: things that fit in a stocking,
cost under $25, and still have a dinosaur on them.

Everything below is a real listing on our store today. Prices, sizes and shipping times come from
those listings. Christmas timing is near the bottom, and it matters, because every item is printed
to order.

## Under $10: stickers and a magnet

**Stickers are the classic dinosaur stocking stuffer**, and the cheapest thing we sell. We print 29
designs in two ranges, all in the [dinosaur stickers collection](/collections/dinosaur-stickers).

The original range is waterproof vinyl that the listings describe as suitable for indoor and outdoor
use, in sizes from 2″ to 6″ square. For a stocking, the seasonal pick is
[Rex's First Snowman](/products/square-stickers), at $3.99 for a 2″ or 3″ sticker. For a child, three
or four small stickers feel like more of a gift than one big one, and they cost about the same.

The newer opaque range is printed on high-opacity vinyl, which covers whatever is underneath. That
makes it the better choice for a teenager's battered laptop or water bottle.
[Synthwave Dinosaur](/products/synthwave-dinosaur-dinosaur-vinyl-stickers) is the one most likely to go
on a laptop: a 3″ is $3.99, and the 15″ × 3.75″ strip is $8.25.

**For adults, the [Winosaur magnet](/products/winosaur-magnet)** is a fridge magnet with a wine joke on
it, $7.99 for a 3″ square up to $9.99 for 6″. It's thin (0.8 mm) with a black backing. It's a good
add-on for someone whose dinosaur phase now comes with a corkscrew.

## Dinosaur stocking stuffer FAQ

### What's the cheapest dinosaur stocking stuffer?

A 2″ original-range sticker. Most are $3.99, and the Happy Birthday T-Rex sticker starts at $3.29.

### Do the socks fit kids?

The listings give one size, fitting women's US 5 up to men's US 12. That suits teenagers and adults.
It will be big on a young child.

### Do the wooden ornaments ship outside the US?

The listing says they ship to the US, Canada, Europe, the UK, Australia and New Zealand only.

### Will a notebook fit in a stocking?

Yes, in a full-size one. The dotted notebooks are 5.5″ × 8.5″, and our Christmas stockings are
13″ × 19.3″.

### Are the ornaments breakable?

The wooden ones are solid Hinoki wood, 9 mm thick, so they'll survive a stocking. The Watercolor Dino
ornament is ceramic, so put it in the toe with something soft around it.

# Dinosaur Tote Bags: 48 Designs, Three Kinds of Bag, and When to Order for Christmas

A dinosaur tote bag is usually a small, sure gift: for the friend who still knows every
dinosaur from the museum trip, the teacher whose classroom is half fossils, or yourself, because
the grocery bag you've been using has a hole in it. The design is the easy part. The useful
questions are how big it is, what it's made of, and whether it will arrive in time.

We sell 48 dinosaur tote bags, all $24.99. They aren't all the same bag. Forty-three are one
15-inch polyester tote with denim handles, four are a cotton-blend tote you can order in different
sizes, and one, the Neon Rex, ships on a much longer clock. Every measurement and lead time below
comes from the live listings.

## The everyday dinosaur tote bag: 43 prints, one bag

Most of the range is the same tote with a different print. It's 15 inches square, made of spun
polyester, and carried on two handles of cotton bull denim, the heavy twill used for work jeans.
The listing rates it at 2.6 gallons (10 litres) and a maximum load of 44 pounds.

In practice that's a library-and-lunch bag. It takes a few hardbacks, a laptop up to about 13
inches, a change of clothes for the gym, or one full grocery run's worth of the heavy stuff. The
handles are 11.8 inches long, which is a hand or forearm carry. It sits under the arm rather than
hanging low off the shoulder, so if you want a bag you can wear over a winter coat, look at the
sized totes below.

Most of the 43 come with a choice of Black, Red or Yellow on the listing, so check the photos for
the version you want. Ten have no option at all, including the four realistic prints (Raptor,
T-Rex, T-Rex Skeleton and Triceratops), Tiny Dino, Camo Dinosaur Sketch and Retro Rainbow
Dinosaurs.

Picking a print is the fun part, and there's a lot to pick from:

- **Bold T-Rex designs:** T-Rex Attack, Angry Rex, Roaring Rex, Half Moon T-Rex, Snow T-Rex and
  SynthRex.
- **Scenes:** [Last of the Dinosaurs](/products/last-of-the-dinosaurs-dinosaur-tote-bag), Dinosaur
  Sunset, Meteor Dinosaurs, Jungle Rex and Pastel Sunrise.
- **Small repeating dinos:** Cute Tiny Dinos, Happy Dinos, Multicolor Dinos and Blue Watercolor
  Dinos. These read as a pattern from across the room and as dinosaurs up close, which suits
  anyone who wants a quieter bag.
- **For the person who knows their dinosaurs:** Dinosaur Chart, Serene Stegosaurus, Mossy
  Avaceratops and two Staurikosaurus designs, a small early dinosaur that doesn't turn up on many
  bags.
- **Retro:** 90s Neon Dinos and Purple 90s Neon Dinos, Purple Lazers and
  Retro Rainbow Dinosaurs.

## The sized dinosaur tote: printed on both sides

Four designs use a different bag: Freedom Forever, American Pride T-Rex, Pink Retro T-Rex and
Stegosaurus Print. These are a 50/50 poly-cotton blend, softer in the hand than the polyester
tote, with the design printed on **both** sides, so it shows whichever way it's facing.

They're the only totes that come in sizes. The
[Pink Retro T-Rex tote](/products/dinosaur-tote-bag-pink-retro-t-rex) and Stegosaurus Print come
in 13, 16 and 18 inches square; Freedom Forever and American Pride T-Rex come in 13 and 16. The
price is $24.99 whichever size you choose, so unless you want a small bag on purpose, the
18-inch is the better value. The 13-inch suits a child carrying a book and a snack.

The listings describe "a set of straps for over-the-shoulder carrying" but don't give a strap
length or a weight rating, so we can't promise a figure for either.

## Which dinosaur bag: tote, purse or backpack?

If you're shopping for a dinosaur bag in general and not set on a tote, the choice comes down to
how it's carried. A tote is open-topped and carried by hand or on the forearm: good for
groceries, books and the beach, not for anything you'd mind falling out. Our
[dinosaur purses](/collections/dinosaur-purses) close up and go over the body, and
[the purse guide](/blogs/blog/dinosaur-purses) compares the crossbody, canvas saddle bag and mini
clutch. For school or a commute with a laptop, the
[dinosaur backpack guide](/blogs/blog/dinosaur-backpacks) covers a full-size bag with a 15-inch
laptop sleeve.

## Dinosaur tote bag FAQ

**How big is a dinosaur tote bag?**
The 43 everyday totes are 15″ × 15″ and hold 2.6 gallons. The four sized totes come in 13″, 16″
or (for two designs) 18″ square.

**Does the tote have a zipper or an inside pocket?**
None of the listings mention a zipper, a closure or an inside pocket, so plan on an open tote.

**Can you wash it?**
The listings don't give care instructions. If it needs cleaning, a gentle cold wash and air dry
is the cautious choice for any printed bag.

**Is the print on both sides?**
On the four sized totes and the Neon Rex, yes. The 43 everyday totes don't say, so assume the
front.

**Will it hold a laptop?**
The 15-inch tote fits a laptop up to about 13 inches, but there's no padded sleeve. For a bigger
laptop, the 18-inch sized tote or a backpack is the safer pick.

**Do you ship outside the US?**
The tote listings state US shipping only.

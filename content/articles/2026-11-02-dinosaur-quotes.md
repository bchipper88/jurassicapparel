# Dinosaur Quotes: 120+ Sayings and Captions for Cards, Kids, Birthdays and Instagram

You've got a card to sign, a cake to pipe, a photo to caption, or a classroom wall that needs something on it, and
"Happy birthday!" isn't going to cut it for a kid who can pronounce *Pachycephalosaurus*. This is our list of
dinosaur quotes for exactly that: the famous movie lines, a few real quotes from writers, and a lot of short sayings

It's sorted by where you'll use it. The quotes credited to a film or a person are real lines. Everything else is

## Famous dinosaur quotes from movies

Movie lines are the first thing most people mean by dinosaur quotes. These are the ones worth knowing, with who
says them, because someone at the party will ask.

### Jurassic Park (1993)

- **"Life, uh, finds a way."** Dr. Ian Malcolm, on why an all-female park won't stay that way. It's the most quoted
  line in the film.
- **"Your scientists were so preoccupied with whether or not they could, they didn't stop to think if they
  should."** Malcolm again, and the line that has outlived the movie. You'll hear it in debates about AI and gene
  editing.
- **"Welcome to Jurassic Park."** John Hammond, as the music swells.
- **"Spared no expense."** Hammond, about nearly everything, including the things that go wrong.
- **"Hold on to your butts."** Ray Arnold (Samuel L. Jackson), as he reboots the park's systems.
- **"Clever girl."** Robert Muldoon, the game warden, in his last moment on screen.
- **"Must go faster."** Malcolm, in the jeep, with the T. rex in the side mirror.
- **"Dinosaurs eat man. Woman inherits the earth."** Ellie Sattler, finishing Malcolm's speech for him.

### The Land Before Time (1988)

The Land Before Time has some of the gentlest lines in any dinosaur film, which is why they turn up on nursery walls.

- **"Some things you see with your eyes. Others you see with your heart."** Littlefoot's mother.
- **"Let your heart guide you. It whispers, so listen closely."** Also Littlefoot's mother. It's the line most often
  used at baby showers and in sympathy cards.
- **"Yep, yep, yep!"** Ducky's catchphrase. Every kid who's seen it will say it back to you.

### The Good Dinosaur (2015)

Searches for *the good dinosaur quotes* mostly come from parents. The film is about Arlo, a young Apatosaurus who's
scared of almost everything, and the line people want is his father's:

- **"Sometimes you gotta get through your fear to see the beauty on the other side."** Poppa, to Arlo. It works for a
  first day of school, a graduation card, or a kid who's nervous about swimming lessons.

### Barney

The *Barney dinosaur quotes* people search for are nearly always the song. Barney's closing number opens with
"I love you, you love me," sung to the tune of "This Old Man." It's the reason a whole generation of parents can't
see a purple dinosaur without hearing it. For a card, it's best quoted as just that first line.

## Real quotes about dinosaurs from writers and scientists

These are less well known than the movie lines and better on a classroom wall or a science-fair poster.

- **"The dinosaurs became extinct because they didn't have a space program."** Usually credited to science-fiction
  writer Larry Niven. The longer version adds that if we go extinct for the same reason, it'll serve us right.
- **"The Age of Reptiles ended because it had gone on long enough and it was all a mistake in the first place."**
  Credited to humorist Will Cuppy, from *How to Become Extinct* (1941), a book of comic essays about animals.
- **"Dinosaurs didn't read. Now they're extinct."** The library poster slogan. Nobody seems to know who wrote it
  first, and every school librarian has used it.

A tip if you're making a poster: pair the quote with a fact. "Dinosaurs ruled for about 165 million years" next to
the space-program line makes a better wall than either one does alone. Our [dinosaur trivia page](/blogs/blog/dinosaur-trivia)
has 80 of those facts with the reasoning behind each.

## Dinosaur quotes FAQ

### What is the most famous dinosaur quote?

"Life, uh, finds a way," said by Dr. Ian Malcolm (Jeff Goldblum) in *Jurassic Park* (1993). Close behind is
Malcolm's line about scientists being "so preoccupied with whether or not they could, they didn't stop to think if
they should," which gets quoted well outside dinosaur conversations.

### What does "rawr" mean in dinosaur?

"Rawr means I love you in dinosaur" is an internet saying from the 2000s that stuck. It's now one of the most popular
lines for baby clothes and Valentine's cards. Real dinosaurs probably didn't roar at all. Many scientists think some
made closed-mouth booming or cooing sounds, more like a crocodile or an ostrich than a movie T. rex.

### What is a good short dinosaur quote for a kid?

"Stay wild, little dino," "Tiny but mighty," and "You're dino-mite" all work. For something with more meaning,
"Every big dinosaur was once a little egg" is good for a new sibling or a first day of school.

### What are the best quotes from The Good Dinosaur?

The one people search for is Poppa's line to Arlo: "Sometimes you gotta get through your fear to see the beauty on
the other side." It's about being scared and doing it anyway, which is why parents use it for first days and
swimming lessons.

### Can I put these quotes on a shirt or card?

For a homemade card, a cake or a social post, yes. The sayings on this page without a credit are ours, and you're
welcome to use them. Movie lines are different: they belong to the studios, so they're fine to quote but not to print
on something you're selling.

### Are there dinosaur quotes about books and reading?

"Dinosaurs didn't read. Now they're extinct" is the classic, from library posters. For a gentler version for little
kids: "Read like a dinosaur: one big bite at a time."

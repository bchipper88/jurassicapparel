# Dinosaur Nursery Decor: Soft Prints, a Rug and a Reading Chair (and What Stays Out of the Crib)

Good dinosaur nursery decor has a strange brief. It has to delight a parent at 3 a.m., calm a baby who
can't focus past arm's length yet, and survive the day that baby becomes a toddler who wants *real*
dinosaurs. The trick is to put the theme on the walls, the floor and the feeding chair, and to keep
the crib itself plain. This guide walks through each of those, with what we actually make for each
spot and three rooms priced out.

## First, the crib stays bare

This is the one rule that isn't about taste. Safe-sleep guidance from the American Academy of
Pediatrics is a firm mattress and a fitted sheet, with no pillows, blankets, bumpers or soft toys in
the crib for a baby's first year. So anything soft and dinosaur-shaped belongs somewhere else in the
room: the rocking chair, the floor, a shelf.

That actually makes a nursery easier to design. The crib becomes a quiet block of white or wood, and
the dinosaurs live on the walls around it. We don't make crib sheets or crib bedding, and we won't
pretend a duvet cover belongs in a crib. Our [dinosaur duvet covers](/collections/dinosaur-bedding)
start at Twin, which is the bed they'll move into later.

## The floor: a rug for tummy time

The floor is where a baby spends their awake hours, so a rug earns its place. The
**[Dino Jungle area rug](/products/dino-jungle-dinosaur-area-rug)** is polyester chenille with hemmed
edges, a one-sided print and a grey underside. It comes in 24″×36″ ($59.99) or 36″×60″ ($99.99).

Be honest with yourself about the print: Dino Jungle is a dense, bold green jungle with roaming
dinosaurs. It suits a nursery painted white or pale green, and it will still look right when the
room turns into a toddler room. It does not suit a blush-and-cream pastel scheme; skip it there and
let the walls do the work. Whatever rug you choose, put a non-slip pad under it.

## What we don't sell

So you're not hunting: we don't make crib sheets, crib bumpers (which you shouldn't use anyway),
mobiles, lamps or night lights, curtains, wall decals or wallpaper. For those, choose a plain color
pulled from your wall art. One dinosaur print per surface looks deliberate; dinosaurs on every surface
looks like a party store.

## Dinosaur nursery decor FAQ

**Is a dinosaur nursery a good gender-neutral theme?**
Yes, if you pick the palette rather than the dinosaurs. Pastel Baby Dinos (sage, blush, powder blue)
and Triceratops in the Ferns (greens) both read as neutral. The Dino Jungle rug leans bold rather than
"boy" or "girl."

**Can I put the dinosaur blanket or pillow in the crib?**
Not for the first year. Use the fleece blanket in the stroller, on the floor or on your lap, and keep
pillows on the chair or a shelf.

**Do you sell dinosaur crib bedding?**
No. Our duvet covers start at Twin size, for the next bed.

**What frame size do the printables need?**
Standard 8″×10″ or 11″×14″. Both sizes are in the download.

**Will the nursery still work when they're a toddler?**
The Dinosaur ABC poster and the Dino Jungle rug are the two pieces that keep going longest. The
pastel printables are the ones you'll eventually swap.

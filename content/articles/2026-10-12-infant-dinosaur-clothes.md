## Newborn dinosaur clothes: start with the size, not the print

This is the part most shoppers get wrong, so it goes first. Our standard bodysuits start at
**3–6 months**, and the size guide on the listing says that size fits a baby 20–25 inches long and
12–16 lbs. Plenty of babies come home smaller than that.

If the baby hasn't arrived yet, you have two good options:

- **Buy the one true newborn size.** The [Best Friends baby dinosaur bodysuit](/products/best-friends-infant-dinosaur-bodysuit)
  is the only piece we make in NB. Its chart lists the NB at 7.25" wide and 10.75" long, and it
  comes in light blue, heather, pink and white.
- **Buy ahead on purpose.** A 3–6m bodysuit is a gift that fits in a few weeks, which is often
  better than one worn twice. For a shower gift, 3–6m or 6–12m is the safer bet.

Here's the full chart from the standard bodysuit listings:

| Size | Width | Fits baby length | Fits baby weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3–6m | 9 3/8" | 20–25" | 12–16 lbs |
| 6–12m | 9 7/8" | 25–28" | 16–22 lbs |
| 12–18m | 10 3/8" | 28–30" | 22–27 lbs |
| 18–24m | 10 7/8" | 30–32" | 27–30 lbs |

When a baby falls between sizes, go by length rather than weight. A bodysuit that's too short
in the body is the one that stops snapping.

## Building a baby shower gift

A gift people remember usually covers the first few months, not just the first week. One way to
put it together:

1. One bodysuit in the size for right now (NB, or 3–6m).
2. One in the next size up, so it still fits at three or four months.
3. A bib, because it'll get used more than either.

That's around $72 for three pieces. If you want something bigger to go with it, a

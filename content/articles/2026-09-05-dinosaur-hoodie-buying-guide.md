# Dinosaur Hoodie Buying Guide: All-Over Print vs. Graphic Print (and How to Size Both)

The mornings went cold about a week ago, which is usually when people start looking for
a dinosaur hoodie. Then they open a shop page, scroll fifty of them, and every listing
looks broadly the same — a dinosaur, a hood, a price somewhere between forty and seventy
dollars.

They are not the same. Across our own [dinosaur hoodie collection](/collections/dinosaur-hoodie)
of 57 designs there are really only two kinds of hoodie, and the difference between them
decides the fabric you get, the size you order, the colour you can pick and what you pay.
Once you can tell them apart in the product photo, the choice takes about thirty seconds.

## The two kinds, and how to spot them in two seconds

**All-over print.** The dinosaurs run edge to edge — across the sleeves, over the hood,
round the back, with no blank space. The whole garment is printed as fabric before it's
sewn, which is why the pattern never stops at a seam. Look for a busy, wall-to-wall design
and a listing with **only a size option and no colour choice**. There can't be a colour
choice: the print *is* the colour.

**Graphic print.** One artwork on the chest, sitting on a plain coloured hoodie. Look for
a listing that offers you **a colour and a size** — black, maroon, forest green, military
green, and so on.

That single tell — colour option or no colour option — is the fastest way to know which
kind you're looking at before you read a word of the description.

## Sizing: two different systems that do not line up

This is where most returns come from, so it's worth two minutes.

**All-over print hoodies run 2XS to 6XL** on most designs and are cut to a relaxed unisex
fit. Relaxed unisex means roomy through the body. If you're between sizes and you want it
close-fitting, size down; if you plan to wear a jumper under it in December, don't.

**Graphic hoodies run S to 3XL**, with a few — *Baryonyx*, *Oviraptor*, *Trade Wife For
Nuggies* — going to 4XL and 5XL. These list a real measurement chart rather than a letter,
and it's the one to trust. On the *T-Rex Skeleton* hoodie, for instance:

| Size | Chest width | Length |
|---|---|---|
| S | 20 in | 27 in |
| M | 21 in | 28 in |
| L | 23 in | 29 in |
| XL | 25 in | 30 in |
| 2XL | 26½ in | 31 in |
| 3XL | 28 in | 32 in |

Chest width there is the flat measurement across the garment, not around your chest — so
lay a hoodie you already like face-down, measure armpit to armpit, and match that number.
It beats guessing every time.

**Kids hoodies are sized by age, not by letter.** The
[kids dinosaur hoodie](/collections/kids-dinosaur-hoodie) range runs XXS (1–2 years)
through XL (11–12 years), and the chart is in inches and centimetres on every listing:
XXS is a 14½ in chest and a 16½ in body length, XL is 19⅓ in and 24½ in. If your child is
tall for their age — and dinosaur-aged children usually are — go by the body length rather
than the birthday.

## What you'll pay, and why the price moves

Graphic prints start at **$39.99** and top out around **$50.99**. All-over prints start
at **$54.99** and run to **$67.49**.

Prices on the all-over designs climb with size — *Colorful Dinos* is $54.99 at the small
end and $65.99 at 6XL — because a 6XL genuinely uses more printed fabric. That's not a
surcharge dressed up as sizing; it's the same reason a 6XL costs more to make. Worth
knowing so the number at checkout isn't a surprise.

## Made to order: the part that affects your timing

Every hoodie here is printed after you order it rather than pulled off a shelf. That's a
deliberate choice — it means no pallet of unsold stock going to landfill at the end of the
season — but it does mean a lead time, and the lead time varies by garment.

The listings quote production and shipping separately for US standard shipping. The
all-over print hoodies list roughly 2–4 business days to produce and 3–8 to ship; the
cotton graphic hoodies list about 2–7 to produce and 2–5 to ship; the kids hoodies list
2–7 and 6–7. Call it **one to two and a half weeks door to door**, and check the specific
listing before you count on a date.

Practically: for a Halloween-week hoodie, order by mid-October. For Christmas, order by
the first week of December and stop reading gift guides.

## Questions people actually ask

**Will an all-over print fade in the wash?**
The design is printed into the fabric rather than laid on top of it, which is why the
hood is patterned inside and out. Wash cold and it stays put. The kids hoodies go further
and are explicitly rated not to peel, flake or crack.

**Is a dinosaur hoodie warm enough for winter?**
The all-over prints are, at 308 g/m² with brushed fleece inside. The cotton graphic
hoodies are a three-season weight — good from September to April with a layer under.

**Can I get a matching one for my kid?**
Not in the same design, mostly — the adult and kids ranges are printed on different
garments. What does match is the theme, and several families do exactly this at Halloween;
we wrote about that in
[family dinosaur costume ideas](/blogs/blog/family-dinosaur-costume-ideas).

**Unisex or gendered sizing?**
The all-over prints are cut unisex relaxed. The graphic hoodies are drawn from men's,
women's and youth blanks depending on the design, which is why the size range shifts
between listings. The measurement chart on each listing is the authority.

range, and there's a much wider spread of tees and other pieces across
[dinosaur apparel](/collections/dinosaur-apparel).

If you want warm, bold and unmistakably dinosaur, buy an all-over print and size for a

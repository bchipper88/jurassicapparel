## What a dinosaur necktie from us is made of

Each tie is 100% polyester at 3.10 ounces per square yard (105 gsm). That's a lightweight fabric
with a silky finish, which is what lets a printed design come out bright and sharp. Silk ties are
usually woven, so the pattern is in the threads; ours are printed, which is why a detailed
picture of a raptor is possible at all.

The print is on the front only. The back is plain, with a keeper loop that holds the narrow end
in place so it doesn't swing out from behind the wide one. The end is the classic V point, so it
knots and hangs like any standard tie.

The listing doesn't give a length, a width or care instructions. If you're buying for someone
very tall or someone who prefers a skinny tie, we can't promise a measurement, so check with us
before you order. For cleaning, spot-clean and hang to dry is the cautious choice for any printed
polyester tie.

## Is a dinosaur tie office-appropriate?

That depends on the office, but the print choice makes more difference than you'd think. Blue and
White Raptors, Ancient Relics and Colorful Dinos use small repeating motifs on a calm background,
so from a few feet away they look like an ordinary patterned tie. 90's Retro does not, and isn't
trying to.

Where a dinosaur tie is completely at home: a museum or science job, school photos, a
paleontology talk, a birthday dinner, a themed wedding, or a dinosaur-loving kid's party where
the grown-up wants to join in.

## Dinosaur tie FAQ

**How much is a dinosaur tie?**
All seven of ours are $21.99.

**Is there a T. rex tie?**
None of the seven is a single big T. rex. Most are repeating patterns of several kinds of
dinosaur, and Blue and White Raptors is all raptors; check the product photos for the species
you want.

**Do you make a kids' dinosaur tie or a bow tie?**
No. Each tie is sold in one size, made for adult wear, and we don't currently sell a dinosaur bow
tie or tie bar.

**Is the print on both sides?**
No, the front only. The back is plain, which is normal for a necktie since the back faces your
shirt.

**Is it silk?**
No, it's printed polyester with a silky finish.

**Do you ship outside the US?**
The tie listings state US shipping only.

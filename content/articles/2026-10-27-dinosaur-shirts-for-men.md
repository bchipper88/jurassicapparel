# Dinosaur Shirts for Men: Five Cuts, Real Sizes, and Which One to Buy

and guessing at the fit. Ours aren't all the same shirt. Behind the prints there are five different
garments: a classic cotton tee, a vintage-fit tee that runs big, an all-over print tee, a slim
polo, and a Hawaiian shirt. They differ in fabric, sizing and how long they take to arrive, so
pick the shirt first and the dinosaur second.

Everything below comes from the live product listings. Where a listing doesn't say something, we
say so instead of guessing.

## The classic tee: the one most men should start with

This is the bulk of the range and the safest buy. It's a soft, lightweight 4.2 oz tee in combed,
ring-spun cotton, pre-shrunk, side-seamed and taped shoulder to shoulder so the neck holds its
shape. Solid colors are 100% cotton; heathers are blends, which drape a little more.

Sizing is generous at the top end. Most designs run XS to 4XL, and the newer ones go to 5XL. The
listings print the chart, measured laid flat:

| Size | XS | S | M | L | XL | 2XL | 3XL | 4XL | 5XL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Length (in) | 27 | 28 | 29 | 30 | 31 | 32 | 33 | 34 | 35 |
| Width (in) | 16½ | 18 | 20 | 22 | 24 | 26 | 28 | 30 | 32 |

Width is armpit to armpit. Lay a tee he already likes flat, measure it, and match the numbers.
That beats guessing between L and XL.

The designs on this cut fall into a few groups:

- **Science and fossils.** The [Fossil Hunter tee](/products/fossil-hunter-dinosaur-t-shirt) is a
  vintage dig-site badge: a T-rex skull, a paleontologist's pick and brush, and "FOSSIL HUNTER" and
  "DIG CREW" lettering. Triceratops Head, Oviraptor, Baryonyx, Brontosaurus Fade and North Dakota
  Triceratops are for the man who knows which one is which.
- **Japanese-print style.** [Samurai T-Rex](/products/dinosaur-shirt-samurai-t-rex), Geisha T-Rex,
  Japanese Pterodactyl, Japanese Birds, Pterodactyl Ninja and Ramen T-Rex. These read more like
  art than a joke, so they suit a man who'd wear the shirt to dinner.
- **Funny.** Dinosaurs Never Had Coffee, Rock, Paper, Scissors, and the
  [Trade Wife For Nuggies tee](/products/trade-wife-for-nuggies-mens-dinosaur-t-shirt), which has
  a women's counterpart, Trade Husband For Nuggies, if you're buying a pair. The rest of the nugget
  range is in our [dino nuggets shirt guide](/blogs/blog/dino-nuggets-shirt).
- **T-rex, plain and simple.** Half Moon T-Rex, Dinosaurs Ruled, Dinosaur Volcano and the T-Rex
  tees. Our [T-rex shirt guide](/blogs/blog/t-rex-shirts) goes deeper on these.

## The vintage-fit tee: softer, and it runs big

The second tee is a different blank, easy to spot because its colors are listed as "Solid Black",
"Solid Heavy Metal" and so on. It's 100% combed ring-spun cotton at 4.3 oz, with a ribbed collar
and a tear-away label instead of a scratchy sewn-in tag. The listing says plainly that it **runs
bigger than usual**, so if he's between sizes, go down one.

These are the designs with the most attitude: [Retro Rex](/products/retro-rex), Hip Rex, Chillin
Rex, Possessed Rex, The Last Dinosaur, Pteroble Puns, and two gym jokes, T-Reps and Rex's First
Pullup. Most stop at 2XL or 3XL, so check the size list before choosing a design for a bigger
build. For more in this style, see our [retro dinosaur shirt guide](/blogs/blog/retro-dinosaur-shirts).

## All-over print tees: the pattern goes everywhere

These are printed seam to seam, so the dinosaurs wrap around the whole shirt. The
[Dino Dad all-over tee](/products/dino-dad-dinosaur-t-shirt) is the one whose listing spells out
the fabric: a cotton-touch polyester jersey (93% polyester, 7% spandex in the US) that the listing
says won't fade after washing. It's sized by chest, from XS at 31⅛″ to 2XL at 51⅝″, so measure him
rather than his shirt. Blue and White Raptors, the 4th of July designs and the Dinosaur Nuggies
men's tee are in this group too, all XS–2XL.

Pick this cut when he wants the shirt to be the whole outfit. Skip it if he needs 3XL or up.

- **You don't know his size well:** a classic tee. Widest range, and the chart is on the page.
- **He's 3XL or bigger:** a classic tee in a 5XL design, or a Hawaiian shirt (to 6XL).
- **He wears shirts to work:** a slim polo, one size up.
- **He's a dad:** Piggyback Dino Dad or Cool Dadasaurus Rex.
- **He likes the science:** Fossil Hunter, Baryonyx or Triceratops Head.

## FAQ

**Are these men's shirts or unisex?**
The classic and vintage-fit tees are a straight unisex cut, so they fit men without a separate
men's pattern. If you're shopping for her too, our
[ladies' dinosaur shirt guide](/blogs/blog/ladies-dinosaur-shirts) covers the women's cuts.

**What's the biggest size?**
6XL in the Hawaiian shirts, 5XL in many classic tees, 4XL in most of the rest.

**Are they 100% cotton?**
Both tees are, in solid colors. Heathers are blends. The all-over tees, polos and Hawaiian shirts
are polyester.

**Do you ship outside the US?**
The tee and polo listings give US shipping only. The Hawaiian shirt listing says it ships to the
US, Canada, Europe, the UK, Australia and New Zealand.

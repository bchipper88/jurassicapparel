## Start with the bed

The duvet cover is the decision that makes the others easy. It covers more of the room than anything
else, so its colors become the room's colors. We make eight
[dinosaur duvet covers](/collections/dinosaur-bedding), and they fall into a few clear looks (more on
those below).

What you need to know before you order:

- **Sizes:** Twin (67″×88″), Twin XL (67″×92″), Queen (88″×88″, fits Full/Queen) and King (104″×88″).
  The listing allows for up to 3″ either way. Twin and Twin XL are $94.99, Queen is $109.99 and King is
  $124.99.
- **What's in the box:** the cover only. The **duvet insert and pillowcases aren't included**, so if
  the bed has a plain comforter now, you can usually slip that inside instead.
- **Build:** soft, lightweight polyester microfiber, printed on the front with a white back, and an
  invisible zipper.
- **Care:** machine wash cold. Don't tumble dry with heat, iron or bleach.

That white reverse is handy. Fold the top of the duvet back and you get a clean white band across the
bed, which calms down a busy print.

## Dinosaur room decor FAQ

**What's the best dinosaur bedding for a toddler moving to a big-kid bed?**
A Twin cover in Dino Dreams or Pastel Dinos. Both are bright without being scary, and both use baby
or friendly cartoon dinosaurs.

**Does the duvet cover come with pillowcases?**
No. It's the cover only. The insert and pillowcases aren't included.

**Will a dinosaur room still work when they're ten?**
Choose Sage Triceratops or Museum Dinosaurs for the bed and the room will age well. Swap the pillows
and posters as they grow, since those are the cheap parts.

**Is the rug soft enough to sit on?**
It's polyester chenille with hemmed edges. The 36″×60″ is the size for sitting and playing on; the 24″×36″
is more of a bedside landing spot.

**How do I decorate a dinosaur room for a boy and a girl sharing?**
Use the Colorful Dinosaurs cover on both beds. It's on warm cream with a mix of colors, so neither
side reads as "boys" or "girls". Then let each kid pick their own shaped pillow.

**Can I wash all of it?**
The duvet covers and printed throws are machine-washable (cold for the duvet). The shaped pillows are
sealed with no zipper, so there's no cover to take off and wash separately.

# Dinosaur Tumblers: Our 20 oz and 25 oz Designs, and How They Differ

A **dinosaur tumbler** is the dinosaur gift for the person who already has a dinosaur mug on
their desk and drinks their coffee in the car anyway. We print seven of them, and they come in
two quite different styles: a slim 20 oz insulated tumbler with a slide lid, and a taller 25 oz
travel mug with a handle.

This page covers what separates the two, every design we make in each, how to look after them,
and how long they take to arrive. Everything about materials and care comes from the product
listings.

## The two kinds of dinosaur tumbler

Both are stainless steel, both are $29.99, and both are printed when you order. After that they
part ways.

| | 20 oz tumbler | 25 oz travel tumbler |
|---|---|---|
| **Designs** | African Dinosaur Safari, Pink Retro T-Rex, American T-Rex | Dinosaur Mom, Serene Stegosaurus, Pastel Sunrise, Dinosaurs Never Had Coffee |
| **Build** | Double-walled, vacuum insulated stainless steel | Stainless steel and BPA-free plastic |
| **Lid** | Splash-proof slide lid | Spill-proof plastic lid |
| **Handle** | No | Yes |
| **Cleaning** | Dishwasher safe | Hand-wash only |
| **Car cup holder** | Not stated on the listing | Fits most car cup holders |
| **Size** | 20 oz | 25 oz (739 ml), 7.9″ tall |
| **Price** | $29.99 | $29.99 |

The short version: **the 20 oz is the one to pick if it's going in the dishwasher**, and its
listing is the one that says vacuum insulated. **The 25 oz is the commuter's cup**: it holds
more, it has a handle, and the listing says it fits most car cup holders. It's hand-wash only,
which matters more than people expect for something used every day.

## The 20 oz insulated dinosaur tumblers

Three designs, each with a clear slide lid and no handle. These are the
listings that use the word "insulated": double-walled stainless steel, an electro-polished
interior, dishwasher safe, BPA free. Don't microwave them.

- **[African Dinosaur Safari](/products/dinosaur-tumblr-african-dinosaur-safari)**: the map of
  Africa filled with a sunset in bands of yellow, orange, red and purple, with dinosaur and
  acacia-tree silhouettes walking across it. The most colorful tumbler we make.
- **[Pink Retro T-Rex](/products/dinosaur-tumblr-pink-retro-t-rex)**: a black T. rex skeleton
  on a pink background with mint blocks, a pink brushstroke and yellow dots. It's the T rex
  tumbler for someone whose taste runs more pastel than Jurassic.
- **[American T-Rex](/products/dinosaur-tumblr-american-t-rex)**: a roaring T. rex painted in
  the stars and stripes, on a light grey tumbler. It also sits in our
  America dinosaurs range, so it works as a Fourth of July gift as well as a Christmas one.

## The 25 oz dinosaur travel tumblers

These are taller, with a handle and a clear plastic lid, and they hold 25 oz. The listing
describes them as a travel mug, which is the honest name: this is the one that rides in the car
and sits on the desk all morning.

- **[Dinosaurs Never Had Coffee](/products/dinosaurs-never-had-coffee-dinosaur-tumbler)**: a
  fossil T. rex skull between two steaming cups, and the line "Dinosaurs never had coffee, and
  we know how that turned out." The same artwork is on one of our
  [dinosaur mugs](/blogs/blog/dinosaur-mugs-guide) if you want a matching pair for home and the
  commute. This listing also offers a **40 oz option at $30.99**. The specs on the listing
  describe the 25 oz version only, so check the option before you order.
- **[Dinosaur Mom](/products/dinosaur-mom-dinosaur-travel-mug)**: "MOM" in big black letters,
  with a triceratops and two pterosaurs cut out of them in white. It's part of the
  [Mamasaurus collection](/collections/mamasaurus), which has more gifts for mothers in the same
  spirit if you're building a bigger gift.
- **[Serene Stegosaurus](/products/serene-stegosaurus-dinosaur-tumbler)**: a lavender
  stegosaurus in a jungle clearing under a palm tree and a pink-and-blue sky. Soft and
  painterly, and the easiest one here to give to someone whose taste you don't know well.
- **[Pastel Sunrise](/products/pastel-sunrise-dinosaur-tumbler)**: a long-necked, sail-backed
  dinosaur grazing through a flower meadow under a big peach sun, in muted greens and corals.

One thing the 25 oz listings flag that's worth repeating: **open the lid before you put it on a
hot drink**, to stop steam pressure building up.

## Dinosaur tumbler FAQ

### Are the tumblers dishwasher safe?

The 20 oz tumblers are, according to their listings. The 25 oz travel tumblers are hand-wash
only.

### Do they keep drinks hot or cold?

The 20 oz listings describe a double-walled, vacuum insulated tumbler that keeps drinks at the
temperature you want. The 25 oz listings say the travel mug keeps your drink "at just the right
temperature" but don't say it's insulated or give a time, so we won't promise hours of heat for
either.

### Can I microwave a dinosaur tumbler?

No. The 20 oz listings say not to, and both styles are stainless steel.

### Is the design on both sides?

It depends on the design. Pink Retro T-Rex is a pattern that runs around the tumbler, while
Serene Stegosaurus is a single picture on one side. Each listing has photos from more than one
angle, so check those before you choose.

### Do you ship tumblers outside the US?

The shipping estimates on the tumbler listings are for standard US shipping. If you're ordering
from elsewhere, check the options at checkout first.

# Dinosaur Christmas Sweaters: Nine Knitted Designs, and When to Order

An ugly sweater party is a contest nobody admits is a contest, and a dinosaur is a strong entry.
We make nine dinosaur Christmas sweaters, all of them knitted, not printed, and each one tells a
different joke: a T-rex that can't reach the top of the tree, a T-rex losing a fight with the gift
wrap, a pair of pterodactyls standing in for the reindeer. Below is what each design is, which to
pick for which party, the sizes and fabric, and the one date that matters most: these are knitted
to order overseas, so they need ordering weeks earlier than most Christmas clothes.

## Which dinosaur Christmas sweater?

All nine use the same knit, the same unisex fit and the same sizes. The design is the only
difference, so pick by the joke and the color.

|---|---|---|---|

Every one has a fair-isle band above the hem and cuffs, and the back and sleeves carry the same
snowflakes or stars as the front, so it still looks like a Christmas sweater from behind.

## For the ugly sweater party

If the point is to get a laugh across the room, pick a design with a punchline.

The [Tree Rex ugly Christmas sweater](/products/tree-rex-adult-dinosaur-ugly-christmas-sweater) is the
one people get from ten feet away: a determined T-rex, tongue out, reaching for the star with arms
that were never going to make it. [Wrap Rex](/products/wrap-rex-adult-t-rex-dinosaur-ugly-christmas-sweater)
is the same joke on a different chore, a T-rex wrapped up in its own curly ribbon, on bright royal
blue. If you're the one who always ends up wrapping the presents, it's the one to wear.

Fa La La La Roar is the loudest of the nine, a raptor mid-carol with its head thrown back and music
notes coming out of its jaws, on burgundy. The

the slow-burn one: it reads as a normal Santa-and-sleigh sweater until someone notices the reindeer
have wings and beaks. On a black knit with gold stars, it's also the darkest of the set.

## For the family photo, the office or Christmas Day

Some sweaters have to survive a photo your mother will frame, or a full day at work. The gentler
designs are cute rather than ridiculous.

Let It Snow puts a brachiosaurus in a striped scarf on a deep violet knit, which looks nothing like
the usual red and green. Merry & Bright, a stegosaurus wearing Christmas lights on a cream
background, is the lightest-colored of the nine. Santa Rex is the classic: a cheerful T-rex in a
Santa hat with a present. Sleighceratops turns a triceratops into a reindeer, red nose and all.

For a group, [Santa Hat Dinos](/products/santa-hat-dinos-adult-dinosaur-ugly-christmas-sweater) is

garland. And because all nine share the same knit and fit, a family or an office team can each pick
a different dinosaur and still look like they planned it.

## Knitted, not printed

This is the part that explains the price. The design on each of these sweaters is jacquard-knitted:
the colors are separate yarns knitted into the fabric, the way a traditional fair-isle sweater is
made, rather than ink printed on the surface. That gives you the textured look a holiday sweater is
supposed to have, and there's no print on top to crack or peel.

What the listings say about the knit:

- 55% cotton, 45% polyester
- Fabric weight 13.27 oz/yd² (450 g/m²); the listings call it a lightweight, soft-feel knit
- Unisex classic fit
- Machine washable at 30–40°C (86–104°F)
- May shrink slightly after the first wash

If you want something festive that's softer, cheaper and quicker to arrive, our printed Christmas
sweatshirts are a different garment, fleece rather than knit, from $34.99. We compared them in our
[guide to dinosaur sweatshirts](/blogs/blog/dinosaur-sweatshirts), and they're in the same collection.

## Sizes, fit and price

All nine sweaters come in nine sizes, 3XS through 3XL. They're $79.99 in most sizes and up to
$83.99 in the largest.

The fit is unisex and classic. The store's advice is to order your usual size for a relaxed look,
and to size up if you're between sizes or plan to wear a shirt underneath. Check the size chart on
the listing before ordering. Because the knit can shrink a little in its first wash, sizing up is
the safer mistake.

There's no children's knit sweater in the range yet. The smallest size is an adult 3XS.

## T-Rex shirt FAQ

**What's the biggest size?**
5XL, in Cool Dadasaurus Rex, T-Rex Winter Forest and T-Rex or Treat. The plain T-Rex, Half Moon and
Samurai tees go to 4XL; Rex's First Pullup, Chillin Rex and Christmas Arms Rex stop at 3XL.

**Do they run true to size?**
Rex's First Pullup, Chillin Rex and Christmas Arms Rex say they run bigger than usual. The plain
T-Rex, Half Moon, Samurai and kids' listings include a size chart; compare it against a shirt that
already fits.

**Can I get adult and kids' T-Rex shirts that go together?**
Yes. There's a T-Rex tee under the same name in adult XS–4XL and youth S–XL, and T-Rex or Treat
comes in adult and toddler sizes. Check the photos to make sure the designs match the way you want.

**Is there a long-sleeve T-Rex shirt?**
Not right now; every T-Rex tee here is short-sleeved.

**Do you sell T. Rex band shirts?**
No. If you're after Marc Bolan's T. Rex, you want a band-merch shop. Ours are the dinosaur kind.

If you're buying one T-Rex shirt and want the safe choice, start with the
[plain T-Rex tee](/products/t-rex-adult-dinosaur-shirt): nine colors, XS–4XL, and nobody will ask what
it is.

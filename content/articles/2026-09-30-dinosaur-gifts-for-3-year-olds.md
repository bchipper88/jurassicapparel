# Dinosaur Gifts for 3 Year Olds: What to Wear, Snuggle and Carry (and What to Skip)

A three-year-old who loves dinosaurs already owns plastic dinosaurs. Probably a bucket of them.
So when you're hunting for dinosaur gifts for 3 year olds, the thing that tends to land is
something they can *be* a dinosaur kid in: a shirt they refuse to take off, a blanket with a
hood they can roar from, a pillow shaped like a baby T. rex.

That's what we make, so that's what this list covers. We don't sell toys. Everything below
comes in a size that fits a three-year-old, and every size, price and shipping time is taken

## What to skip at three (and save for four)

**Backpacks.** Our [dinosaur backpacks](/collections/dinosaur-backpack) are proper school bags:
the standard one is 16⅞″ tall and 12¼″ wide, with a padded 15″ laptop sleeve. On most
three-year-olds that bag hangs well below their bottom. The exception is the
[Neon Rex backpack](/products/neon-rex-backpack), which comes in a **Child size for ages 4 to 7**.
That's the one to give at four, for the first year of pre-K.

**Shoes.** The [kids dinosaur shoes](/collections/dinosaur-shoes-for-kids) start at
**11 Child (EU 28)**. That's bigger than many three-year-olds wear, so check the size on their
current shoes before you order. For a big-footed four or five-year-old they're a great gift, and
slip-ons start at $49.99. Our [dinosaur shoe guide](/blogs/blog/dinosaur-shoes-buying-guide)
has the full size chart.

## FAQ

### What size should I buy a three-year-old?

For shirts and dresses, 3T is the usual size. If you know their weight, the cotton toddler tee
lists 3T at 33–36 lbs. For hoodies, pick XS (3/4 years). When in doubt, go up a size.

### Can they sleep with the pillow or blanket?

These are decorative pillows and throw-style blankets, not sleep products. Follow the same
bedtime rules you'd use for any pillow or blanket at their age.

### Do you sell dinosaur toys?

No. We make things to wear and things for their room. If they already have the toy
dinosaurs, a shirt with their favourite one on it is usually the gift that gets used most.

### Can I get the same print on more than one thing?

Sometimes. The Dino Friends print comes as a kids hoodie, a hooded blanket and a pillow, and the
Abstract Dino Forest print comes as a toddler tee and a youth hoodie. Search the print name on
the store to see everything it comes on.

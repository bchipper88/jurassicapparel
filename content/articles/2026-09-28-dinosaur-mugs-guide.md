# Dinosaur Mugs: Which Size to Pick, Which Designs Come in 20 oz, and When to Order

Most people shopping for a **dinosaur mug** already know who it's for. There's the coworker
with a T. rex on their desk, the dad who calls himself Dadasaurus without irony, and the kid
who's decided they drink hot chocolate now. The design is the easy part. The size is harder,
and so is working out whether it will turn up in time.

We print 84 dinosaur mug designs. This page covers the three sizes and what each one holds,
which designs come in the big 20 oz, the color-changing mugs, the Christmas mugs, and how long
shipping takes.

## What every mug has in common

These details come from the listings and are the same across the range:

- **White ceramic** with a **C-shaped handle**
- Holds **hot and cold** drinks
- **Printed when you order**, which is why it takes a few days to ship (more on that below)

Prices go by size, not design: **$19.99 for 11 oz, $21.99 for 15 oz, $23.99 for 20 oz** on
nearly every design. The color-changing mugs are the exception, and they're covered further down.

## 11, 15 or 20 oz: picking a dinosaur mug size

The number on the listing is how much the mug holds filled to the brim. In practice you fill
a mug an inch or so short of that, so it's worth knowing what each one really takes.

| Size | Roughly holds | Good for | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| **11 oz** | One standard cup of coffee with room for milk | Tea, a morning coffee, kids' hot chocolate, smaller hands | $19.99 |
| **15 oz** | A café "tall" with space to spare | Anyone who refills before 10 a.m. | $21.99 |
| **20 oz** | Close to two cups | The person who treats their mug like a thermos | $23.99 |

The volumes are our rough guide; the prices are from the listings.

If you're buying a gift and don't know their habits, **15 oz is the safe middle**. It's big
enough that no one feels short-changed without being unwieldy. The 11 oz is lighter and easier for a child to hold. The 20 oz is a statement, and
the people who want one are very sure they want one.

Three designs are "disappearing" mugs: **Retro Rex**, **Possessed Rex** and **Raptor
Watching**. In the photos the mug is black when it's empty, and the design shows once a hot

something happens when you pour.

Two things to know. They come in fewer sizes (11 oz, with 15 oz on two of the three), and the
price varies: [Retro Rex](/products/retro-rex-color-changing-mug) is $24.99 for 11 oz, while
Possessed Rex is listed at the standard $19.99. Two of the three are grouped in the

## How long a dinosaur mug takes to arrive

Every mug is printed to order. The listings give these estimates for **standard US shipping**:

| Stage | Estimate |
|---|---|
| Production | 1–2 business days |
| Shipping | 2–5 business days |
| **Door to door** | **3–7 business days** |

That makes mugs one of the fastest things we sell. For Christmas, working back seven business
days from a **Wednesday, December 23** delivery gives an order-by date of **Monday,
December 14**. Carriers slow down in the last two weeks before Christmas, so treat that as
the last safe day, not the target. If you're also ordering a
[dinosaur stocking](/blogs/blog/dinosaur-christmas-stockings), order the stocking first,
because it has a much longer lead time than the mug.

## Dinosaur mug FAQ

### Are the mugs dishwasher and microwave safe?

The listings don't say, so we won't promise either here. Hand-washing is the safe choice for
any printed mug if you want the design to stay bright. This matters most for the
color-changing mugs, which rely on a heat-sensitive coating.

### Is the print on both sides?

It depends on the design. Patterned prints like the Christmas mugs cover the mug, while
single-image designs like Dadasaurus Rex put one piece of artwork on the front. The product
photos show which kind each one is.

### Do you ship dinosaur mugs outside the US?

The shipping estimates on the mug listings are for standard US shipping. If you're ordering
from elsewhere, check the options at checkout before you order.

### Which dinosaur mug is best for a kid?

Go for the 11 oz: it's lighter and easier to hold. Tea-Rex Time is the most cartoonish design,
and a color-changing mug adds a bit of magic to the first hot chocolate. Ceramic is still
ceramic, though, so it's a mug for the table, not the car.

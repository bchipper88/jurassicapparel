## The cotton nuggie tees: the safe pick

Three designs share one blank: a 100% combed, ring-spun cotton tee at 4.2 oz per square yard,
pre-shrunk, side-seamed, with shoulder-to-shoulder taping. It's light, soft and has a little
give. Heather colors contain polyester, so pick a heather if you want a bit more drape and a
solid if you want pure cotton.

- **[Powered By Dino Nuggies](/products/powered-by-dino-nuggies-dinosaur-t-shirt)**, from

  someone and don't know their taste, this is the one.

The size range is the main reason to start here. XS to 5XL covers nearly every adult. Trade

## The all-over-print dino nugget shirt for men

The **[Dinosaur Nuggies men's tee](/products/dinosaur-nuggies-mens-t-shirt)** ($34.99–$36.99) is
a different garment. It's a cotton-touch polyester jersey (93% polyester, 7% spandex in the US)
with the print covering the whole shirt rather than a graphic on the chest. The listing says it
won't fade in the wash, which is the point of printing on polyester.

Two things to know before you buy:

1. **It stops at 2XL.** The chart runs from a 31⅛" chest at XS to 51⅝" at 2XL. Anyone bigger
   should get one of the cotton tees above, which go to 5XL.
2. **It takes longest to arrive.** 2–5 business days to make, then 7–12 to ship.

If you want the nuggets everywhere rather than in one spot, this is the shirt. If you want the
softest shirt, it isn't.

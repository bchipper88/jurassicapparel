# Dinosaur Blankets: Throw, Hooded or Woven, Which Size, and When to Order for Christmas

A dinosaur blanket gets used harder than almost anything else in the house. It gets dragged to
the sofa, wrapped round a shivering kid after bath time, left on the floor and sat on by the dog.
So the print is what makes you want it, but the fabric, the size and how it washes decide whether
it's still on the sofa next winter.

## The printed throw: the everyday dinosaur blanket

This is most of the range: seven designs on the same blanket. It's 100% polyester with a
**soft, silk-touch finish**, printed on one side with a **plain white reverse**. It's
machine-washable and hypoallergenic, which matters for a blanket that lives on a kid's bed.

Be clear about what it is: a smooth, light throw, not a thick fleece. It's the blanket you pull
over your legs watching a film, not the one you'd take camping in November. If warmth is the
point, skip down to the hooded blankets.

### Which size

The throw comes in two sizes, and the right one depends on who's under it.

- **50″ × 60″ ($44.99):** the classic throw size. It covers one adult from shoulders to knees on
  the sofa, or a child pretty much head to toe. It folds over the arm of a couch without taking
  over the room.
- **60″ × 80″ ($59.99):** big enough for an adult to stretch out under, for two people to share,
  or to lay across the top of a twin bed as a cover. If you're buying for a tall adult, or you
  want it on a bed rather than a sofa, get this one.

rest round you like a cape, and keep your hands free for a book, a controller or a mug. Kids
treat them as part blanket, part costume. Adults wear them at a cold desk and won't admit it.

## Dinosaur blankets for adults vs kids

Only one blanket in the range is drawn for small children; the rest work as well on an adult's
sofa as in a kid's room. A few pointers:

- **For a young child:** the Youth Dino Friends hooded blanket. The cartoon dinosaurs are drawn for
  them, and they can wear it. For a bed, a 50″ × 60″ throw in Crazy 90's or SynthRex.
- **For an adult who loves dinosaurs but has a grown-up living room:** Serene Stegosaurus, Mossy
  Avaceratops or Dinosaur Array in 60″ × 80″, or the woven throw.
- **For an adult who wants to be warm:** the adult Dino Friends with the sherpa lining.
- **For two people on one sofa:** the 60″ × 80″ throw. The 50″ × 60″ isn't a sharing blanket.

## Care

The printed throws are machine-washable. As a general rule, printed polyester keeps its color
longest washed cool and dried on low, and high heat is what flattens a sherpa lining. The throws
are printed on one side only, so the white reverse will show any stains first. Keep it face-up on
a sofa that sees snacks.

## Dinosaur blanket FAQ

**Are the throw blankets warm?**
They're light. The silk-touch polyester is soft and drapes well, but it isn't fleece. For real
warmth, choose a hooded blanket with the sherpa lining.

**What size is a dinosaur throw blanket?**
Ours come in 50″ × 60″ (a standard throw) and 60″ × 80″ (big enough for an adult to stretch out
under, or to share).

**Is the print on both sides?**
No. The throws are printed on one side with a white reverse.

**Can I put it in the washing machine?**
The printed throws are machine-washable. Washing cool and drying low keeps the colors bright.

**What's the difference between youth and adult hooded blankets?**
Size. Dino Friends is 60″ × 41″ (youth) or 80″ × 55″ (adult), both $59.99. Hover Board is
60″ × 45″ ($59.99) or 80″ × 60″ ($79.99).

**Do you ship outside the US?**
The throw and Dino Friends listings say US only.

# Dinosaur Notebooks: Five Covers, Dotted or Ruled Pages, and What Each One Is For

A **dinosaur notebook** is one of the few dinosaur gifts that gets used every day without
anybody having to wear it. We print five designs. Four are the same notebook inside, with 140
dotted pages, and differ only in the cover. The fifth is our original design, with 120 ruled
pages, and it suits a different kind of writer.

This page covers what's inside each one, what's on every cover, who each suits, and how long they
take to arrive. All the specifications come from the product listings. The cover descriptions
come from looking at the photos.

## Dotted or ruled: the one decision that matters

Choose the paper first, then the dinosaur. The two kinds of page suit different people.

| | Dotted notebooks (4 designs) | Ruled notebook (1 design) |
|---|---|---|
| Pages | 140 dotted pages | 120 ruled single pages |
| Size | 5.5″ × 8.5″ (US-fulfilled) | Not stated on the listing |
| Binding | Metal wire-o spiral | Spiral |
| Cover | Soft-touch coating | Printed front, black back |
| Price | $22.99 | $22.99 |
| Best for | Journaling, sketching, lists, bullet journals | School notes, lined writing |
| Designs | Serene Stegosaurus, Serene Pastel Stegosaurus, Minimalist Dino, Pastel Sunrise | Jurassic Fuji |

**Dotted pages** are a grid of faint dots instead of lines. You can write on them as if they
were ruled, but you can also draw, make tables or lay out a weekly planner without lines getting in
the way. They're what most people mean when they say "journal" now, and they're the better gift
when you don't know exactly how someone will use it.

**Ruled pages** are the familiar lined paper. For a student taking class notes, or anyone who just
wants to write in straight lines without thinking about it, the ruled notebook is the simpler pick.

## The four dotted dinosaur notebooks

All four are the same notebook: 140 dotted pages, a metal wire-o spiral binding, and a cover with a
soft-touch coating. US orders are 5.5″ × 8.5″, which is about the size of a paperback and fits in a
bag without taking it over. Only the cover changes.

**[Serene Pastel Stegosaurus](/products/serene-pastel-stegosaurus-spiral-notebook).** The same

**[Minimalist Dino](/products/minimalist-dino-dinosaur-notebook).** The plainest cover we make, and

**[Pastel Sunrise](/products/pastel-sunrise-dinosaur-notebook).** A sail-backed dinosaur in sage

## Which dinosaur notebook for which person

- **A child who draws:** Serene Stegosaurus. A dotted page doesn't fight a drawing the way lines do,
  and the cover is the brightest we print.
- **A student:** Jurassic Fuji, for the ruled pages.
- **Someone who keeps a journal or a bullet journal:** any of the dotted four. Pick by taste: Serene
  Pastel Stegosaurus or Pastel Sunrise for soft colors, Minimalist Dino for something understated.
- **A colleague, or for your own desk at work:** Minimalist Dino. It's the one that doesn't look like
  a children's notebook.
- **A dinosaur journal for a gift where you don't know their habits:** go dotted. It works as lined
  paper and as blank paper, so it's hard to get wrong.

## Dinosaur notebook FAQ

### What are dotted pages?

Pages printed with a light grid of small dots instead of lines. You can write on them like lined
paper, but they also work for sketches, lists and planner layouts. The four soft-touch notebooks
have 140 of them.

### Are there lined dinosaur notebooks?

Yes, one. Jurassic Fuji has 120 ruled pages.

### How big are the notebooks?

The four dotted notebooks are 5.5″ × 8.5″ when fulfilled in the US (5.7″ × 8.5″ from the EU). The
Jurassic Fuji listing doesn't state a size.

### Will the spiral lie flat?

The dotted notebooks use a metal wire-o binding, a double-loop spiral that lets a notebook open flat
or fold back on itself. That makes them easier to write in at a desk or on your lap.

### Can I personalize a dinosaur notebook?

None of the notebook listings offers a personalization option. Each design is printed as shown.

### Do you ship notebooks outside the US?

The listings give US shipping estimates and mention EU fulfilment for the dotted notebooks. If you're
ordering from outside the US, check the options at checkout.

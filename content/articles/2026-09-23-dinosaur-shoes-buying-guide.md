*kind* of shoe, what size, and whether it will actually arrive before the birthday or the
holidays. Every pair in our range is printed to order, so those three questions matter more
than they would for a box off a warehouse shelf.

This guide covers all of it — the four shoe styles we make, how our men's, women's and kids'
sizes line up with each other, what each costs, and the date you need to order by. Every
spec below is read off the live product listings.

### High tops: the statement shoe

There are 28 designs in the [dinosaur high tops collection](https://jurassicapparel.com/collections/dinosaur-high-tops),
and they're the pair most people picture when they think of us. The canvas is printed on
both sides, so the pattern wraps the whole shoe rather than sitting on a panel. Many
designs let you choose white or black trim, which changes the look more than you'd expect —
black reads darker and more grown-up, white lets the print carry the shoe.

One thing to check before you fall for a design: about a third of the high tops are offered
in women's sizes only. *Shadow T-Rex*, *Raptor Watching*, *Space Dinos* and *Angry Rex*, for
example, come in both men's and women's sizes; *Pink Plaid Dinos* and *Fun Dinos* are
women's only. The size menu on each listing tells you which.

Pick high tops if the shoe *is* the outfit. They're the most visible thing we make, and at
$79.99 they're the pair people buy as the big gift.

### Low tops: same print, easier to wear

The low tops share the high tops' canvas, double-sided print, lace-up closure and metal
eyelets, with an EVA outsole. They're the better choice for someone who wants the design
without the ankle — easier over trousers, easier in summer, and less of a commitment for
someone trying dinosaur shoes for the first time.

### Mesh sneakers: the one the whole family can match

The mesh sneakers are a different shoe altogether: a lightweight, breathable mesh upper
with a lace-up closure and an EVA sole. This is where you'll find most of the
[kids' dinosaur shoes](https://jurassicapparel.com/collections/dinosaur-shoes-for-kids).

Here's the useful detail that isn't obvious from the titles: **several sneaker designs

*Cool T-Rex*, *Colorful Dino Skeletons*, *Mixed Up Dino's* and *Jurassic Stencil* all do. If

The flip side: a product's title doesn't always tell you its size range. Some sneakers are

## Dinosaur shoe sizing: how the three size runs line up

Every size on our shoe listings carries its EU number, and that's the key to getting the
fit right. Men's and women's labels are just two names for the same underlying sizes:

| EU | Women's | Men's |
|---|---|---|
| 38 | US 7 | US 5 |
| 39 | US 8 | US 6 |
| 40 | US 9 | US 7 |
| 41 | US 10 | US 7.5 |
| 42 | US 11 | US 8.5 |
| 43 | US 11.5 | US 9.5 |
| 44 | US 12 | US 10 |

Three practical consequences:

1. **A women's US 8 and a men's US 6 are the same shoe (EU 39).** Buying for someone who
   normally wears the other gender's sizing? Match the EU number, not the label.
2. **Men's sizes skip some half and whole sizes.** The run goes 7, 7.5, 8.5, 9.5, 10 — there
   is no men's US 8 or 9. If you wear a 9, check the size chart image on the listing and
   compare your foot length rather than guessing up or down.
3. **The kids' run is child 11 to youth 3 (EU 28–34).** There are no toddler sizes. If
   you're shopping for a very small child, this range won't fit yet.

The high-top, low-top and sneaker listings carry the store's **Perfect Fit Guarantee**:
if your pair arrives too big or too small, let us know and we'll replace it free, up to a
two-size difference. That takes most of the risk out of ordering a size you've never tried.

## When to order dinosaur shoes for Christmas (and Halloween)

This is the part people get wrong. Our shoes are made to order, and each listing gives two
phases — production, then shipping:

| | Production | Shipping | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Standard** | 3–7 business days | 10–15 business days | 13–22 business days |
| **Express** | 3–7 business days | 4–7 business days | 7–14 business days |

Tracking numbers are sent once your order moves into the shipping phase, so a few quiet
days after ordering is normal.

Working back from the slow end of those estimates, and skipping Thanksgiving:

- **For Christmas, order standard shipping by about November 20**, or express by about
  December 3.
- **For Halloween, order standard shipping by about September 30** — which is soon. The
  limited-edition [*T-Rex or Treat* high tops](https://jurassicapparel.com/products/t-rex-or-treat)
  are the obvious pick for that one.

Those dates are arithmetic on the store's own estimates, not a guarantee from the carrier.
Earlier is better.

**The exception: flip-flops.** Our four dinosaur flip-flop designs have a much shorter
timeline — 2–3 business days of production and 3–6 of shipping — and ship within the US
only. They have a rubber sole with a soft polyester fabric lining and come in S, M and L.

## Dinosaur shoes FAQ

**Are the men's and women's shoes different shoes?**
Where a design comes in both, it's the same print offered in two size labels, and each size
shows its EU equivalent. Match the EU number rather than the label.

**Do you make toddler dinosaur shoes?**
Not currently. The smallest size is child 11 (EU 28).

**What if the size is wrong?**
High tops, low tops and sneakers carry the Perfect Fit Guarantee: we'll replace a pair that's
too big or too small free, up to a two-size difference.

**Why does shipping take longer than a big-box store?**
Every pair is printed after you order rather than pulled from stock. It avoids producing
shoes nobody buys, but it means you should plan around the production window above.

**Are these waterproof?**
Nothing on the listings says so, and we won't claim it. The high tops and low tops are canvas
and the sneakers are mesh — treat them like any canvas or mesh sneaker in the rain.

# Family Dinosaur Costume Ideas That You'll Actually Wear Again

Every year the same thing happens. You spend $180 on four polyester costumes in early
October. On the 31st it's 44°F, so everyone wears a winter coat over them and nobody can
tell what anyone is. By November 2nd all four are in a bag in the garage.

A family dinosaur costume is one of the few themes that lets you skip that entirely.
Dinosaurs read instantly — nobody needs the costume explained — and the pieces that make
one work are hoodies, onesies and bodysuits you'd buy anyway. Here's how to build one for
each member of the family, including the toddler who will refuse to wear anything scratchy
and the adult who wants to be comfortable on the couch by 8pm.

## Start here: pick your family's dinosaur *type*, not matching outfits

The mistake most families make is buying five identical things. It looks like a uniform,
and it means the six-year-old and the grandmother are wearing the same graphic.

Themed variation works better and it's what actually photographs well:

- **A herd** — everyone is a different dinosaur. Stegosaurus, T-Rex, Brontosaurus,
  Triceratops. Different prints, one obvious theme.
- **The family tree** — Mamasaurus, Dadasaurus, and the little ones. This is the easiest
  one to pull off because the products literally say it on the front.
- **Full prehistoric** — one adult goes all-in on a full dinosaur onesie, everyone else
  wears dino prints. The contrast is the joke.

Mix all three if you want. The theme carries it.

Why a hoodie instead of a costume:

1. **It goes over a long-sleeve shirt.** Layering is the whole game on a cold night.

Skip the inflatable T-Rex suits. They're funny for about ninety seconds, then whoever's
wearing one can't hold a candy bucket, can't fit through a doorway, and can't get in the
car. They also need a battery pack running the whole night.

## Frequently asked questions

**How far ahead should I order?**
Order by early October. Standard production and shipping needs about two weeks, and the
last week of October is when sizes start going. If you're reading this in late August,
you're ahead — that's the right time.

**Do the adult onesies run true to size?**
The Realistic Jurassic onesie runs 2XS–6XL and is cut as sleepwear, so it's roomier than
a fitted garment. If you're between sizes and want to layer underneath, size up.

**Can we match if some of us want shirts and some want hoodies?**
Yes, and it looks better than everyone in the identical item. Keep the theme consistent —
all Mamasaurus/Dadasaurus family graphics, or all dinosaur prints — and mix the garment
types freely.

**Will kids' hoodies fit over a coat?**
Other way around. Put the hoodie on over a long-sleeve base layer, then a coat over
everything if it's genuinely cold. On most Halloween nights a hoodie plus a base layer is
enough on its own, which is the point.

**What about the dog?**
[Dinosaur dog bandanas](/collections/dinosaur-dog-bandanas) exist. We are not going to
pretend this is a serious costume decision, but it does complete the photo.

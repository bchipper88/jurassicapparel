who wants a T. rex on the tree, maybe it's you. Either way you're now thinking about more
than one ornament: the stockings, the paper under the tree, something to wear on Christmas
morning, and whether any of it will arrive in time.

This page is the plan. It covers everything we make for a dinosaur Christmas, the one trick
that makes a themed house look deliberate rather than random, and a single table of order-by
dates, because the pieces ship on very different clocks.

## Dinosaur Christmas order-by dates

Everything is made to order and ships from within the US (the listings say *Standard Shipping,
US only*). Each product has its own production and shipping estimate, and the gap between them
is big enough that one "order by" date for the whole house would be wrong.

The dates below use the **slowest** estimate on each listing, counted in business days, for
arrival by **Wednesday, December 23**. Christmas Day is a Friday this year.

| Product | Door to door (listing) | Order by, for Christmas |
|---|---|---|
| Stockings | 12–35 business days | **Tuesday, November 3** |
| Toddler dresses | 7–15 business days | **Wednesday, December 2** |
| Sweatshirts and tees | 4–11 (tee), 5–11 (sweatshirt) business days | **Tuesday, December 8** |
| Wooden ornaments | 4–10 business days | **Wednesday, December 9** |
| Socks | 6–10 business days | **Wednesday, December 9** |
| Wrapping paper | 5–9 business days | **Thursday, December 10** |
| Mugs | 3–7 business days | **Monday, December 14** |

Two practical notes. If the decorations need to be up for the weekend after Thanksgiving,
move the ornament and wrapping paper dates about four weeks earlier (the
[ornaments guide](/blogs/blog/dinosaur-christmas-ornaments-guide) has the exact date).
And carriers slow down in the last two weeks before Christmas, so treat everything in
December as the latest date, not a target.

The simplest plan: **order the stockings in October, everything else in one order by early
December.**

## Frequently asked questions

**What counts as a "dinosaur Christmas"?**
Whatever your dinosaur fan wants it to be. In practice it's usually a tree with a few dinosaur
ornaments, a dinosaur stocking, and presents wrapped in dinosaur paper. Matching the print across
those three is what makes it look like a theme.

**Can I get the ornaments or stockings personalized?**
Not through the listings. Neither the wooden ornaments nor the stockings have a name field. A
paint pen on the back of an ornament or on the stocking cuff works well.

**Do you ship dinosaur Christmas items outside the US?**
The listings describe standard shipping within the US only, and every date on this page assumes
a US address.

**Is there a dinosaur Christmas sweater?**
Not a knitted one. The two Christmas sweatshirts, *T-Rex Winter Forest* and *Merry Little
Rexmas*, are the warm option, and both run from S to 5XL.

**What's the cheapest way to add dinosaurs to Christmas?**
A single $17.99 ornament, or the $19.99 Santa Hat socks as a stocking stuffer. A pack of
wrapping paper ($24.99) wraps about three presents, and every one of them says "dinosaur"
before it's opened.

**What should I order first?**
The stocking. It has the longest shipping window by weeks. Order it by November 3 and the rest
can follow in December.

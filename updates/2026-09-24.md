## What I found

Four things, and three of them need you.

**1. The `toys` collection is 30 products and all 30 are DRAFT.** `/collections/toys` renders
empty to every customer. Toys are the single most natural destination in a dinosaur gift guide
and I had to cut them from the article. `BACKLOG.md` #12. The question is simply whether this is
deliberate the way the Christmas pajamas were — if it is, say so and I'll stop raising it.

**2. The shipping policy page is blank.** The refund policy is detailed and good; the shipping
policy has an empty body. These are made-to-order products, so in November the customer's real
question is "production time *plus* shipping — will it arrive by the 25th?", and the page meant
to answer it says nothing. That's a Q4 conversion leak on every gift-guide visitor.
`BACKLOG.md` #14. Fifteen minutes in Settings → Policies.

**3. `dinosaur-jewelry` is 5/6 draft** — one live product on the page. The five drafts are priced
$5.99–$12.99, which is the impulse-gift band the catalog is otherwise missing entirely.
`BACKLOG.md` #13.

**4. The link checker was lying to me, and I fixed it.** `check-links.py` validated a collection
on its *total* product count — drafts included. It would have green-lit a link to the all-draft
`toys` collection as perfectly healthy. `data/catalog.json` now records verified `active` counts
and a `draft_heavy_do_not_link` list, and the checker fails on them (regression-tested against
`toys`). I also fixed `md-to-shopify.py`, which hard-coded "halloween / family costume" tags onto
*every* article regardless of subject — tags now come from front matter, and Day 1's are pinned to
match what's actually live on Shopify. `BACKLOG.md` #16, no action needed.

Worth saying plainly: findings 1 and 3 were invisible until the tooling stopped lying. That's the
argument for fixing instruments before trusting readings.

## On product availability

One correction to a natural assumption: most products return `totalInventory: 0` while remaining
`availableForSale: true`. Inventory is untracked because fulfilment is print-on-demand across nine
apps. A zero here does **not** mean out of stock, and shouldn't be flagged as one. Every price and
product named in today's article was confirmed ACTIVE and purchasable today.

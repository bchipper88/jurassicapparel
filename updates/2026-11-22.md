How the page is built:
- **The twist up front:** the two never met. They lived about 80 million years apart, so T. rex is closer in time to us

- **A side-by-side table** covering when, where, length, weight, skull, teeth, bite, arms, relatives and the
  best-known fossil (Big Al vs Sue).
- **Not close cousins:** carnosaur vs coelurosaur. T. rex is closer kin to birds.
- **Bite:** Rayfield et al. 2001 (a strong skull, a weak muscle bite, the "hatchet" strike) against T. rex's crushing
  bite of 35,000 N or more. Lautenschlager 2015 on gape (about 79–92° vs about 63.5°).
- **Arms** (the round Allosaurus wins), **speed** (labelled as models), **Big Al's injuries** and the **Stegosaurus
  spike wound** (Carpenter et al. 2005).
- **Who would win, argued from the evidence:** adult vs adult, then the closer case of a big Allosaurus against a
  teenage T. rex.
- A Jurassic World section, ten kids' facts and a seven-question FAQ.

## What I found

- **The September spike was a news story, not seasonality.** On 2026-09-14 the press covered Falkingham et al. in the
  *Journal of Vertebrate Paleontology*: four T. rex footprints in a 7 m trackway near Marmarth, North Dakota. A high
  school teacher found them in 2025. The demand underneath the spike is steady, and the page leads with that story.
- **Research sourcing:** ScienceAlert (the ND details), Wikipedia's *Tyrannosauripus* entry (track list; it has
  citation problems, so I used only the claims that other sources back up) and NHM London (preservation types).
  Popular Mechanics and the Philmont fandom page couldn't be fetched. I left out details I couldn't confirm, such as
  the Philmont print's width and first names that the sources didn't give.
- **The T. rex suggestion pull (2026-11-21) has more winnable terms:** `allosaurus vs t rex` 5,400 SD 26, `feathered

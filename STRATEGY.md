## What October taught us

1. **The commercial well is close to dry at today's inventory.** About thirty articles went live in
   October, most of them product-led Christmas terms. By the last week, the daily sweeps were
   failing ten or more candidates a day, most of them on stock: one beach towel, one magnet, one duffle, no long
   sleeves, no jewelry. A product term we can sell into with a real collection behind it is now
   rare. The articles we have cover almost every collection with depth.
2. **Informational dinosaur content is the open seam.** Puns (Day 40), jokes (Day 41) and trivia
   (Day 42) all have four-figure volume at SD 26–29, and small sites (DA 2–25) rank on page one.
   These pages don't sell directly. They earn traffic and links at a DA where commercial terms are
   getting harder, and they link into kids' apparel, printables and puzzles. This is November's
   main play.
3. **Rescues work when they happen.** The owner fixed the flying-dinosaurs post's title on
   2026-10-14. It went from #13 to **#9 for `flying dinosaurs names` (5,400/mo)**. That's the
   biggest single ranking gain of the month, and it took about ten minutes. Four briefs are still
   waiting (gifts, socks, rug, flying-dinosaurs rewrite). The approval queue, not the writing, is
   now the constraint on growth.
4. **The catalog is moving fast now, and that's good.** About 130 products went live in October:
   Christmas went from 45 to 65 ACTIVE, sweatshirts from 6 to 20 per gender, baby from 35 to 47.
   There are new `dinosaur-gifts` and `black-friday` collections, and the gift collection went
   from 3 to 46. Several "skipped on stock" calls from September are now out of date, so a monthly
   recount is too slow. Re-check stock when a skipped term comes back into season.
5. **Christmas content timing was right, and the verdict is pending.** The Christmas pages went
   live 4–10 weeks ahead of their ramps. Their real test is November–December rank and traffic,
   which the weekly snapshots will show from 2026-11-02.

## What success looks like by 2026-12-01

Honest ranges, with assumptions:

- **Traffic estimate for November: 1,100–1,500.** November 2025 was 1,445, so matching last year
  would mean the year-on-year decline has stopped. If November comes in under 1,100 while the
  Christmas pages are indexed, the Christmas pages aren't ranking, and December's daily slot
  should go to rescue briefs and internal links rather than new pages.
- **Positions 4–10 at 25 or more** (22 on 2026-10-26). This is the leading indicator. It moves
  before traffic does.
- **At least one full rescue approved.** The title-fix result says rescues are the highest-return
  work available. If none is approved by December, the agent should shrink each brief further,
  down to one-line title and meta changes the owner can make in two minutes.

Assumptions: no algorithm shock, one article a day sustained, publishing stays unattended (it has
worked on recent runs), and the Ubersuggest daily quota holds (#63).

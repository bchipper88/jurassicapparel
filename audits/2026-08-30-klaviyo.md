**Data source:** Klaviyo API, trailing 12 months unless noted. Account figures are exact.

## Headline

**Klaviyo generated $1,092.08 and 15 orders in the last 12 months, from 14,316 sends.**

| Source | Sends | Orders | Revenue |

| Email Welcome Series | 1,475 | 11 | $868.03 |

| Browse Abandonment | 15 | 0 | $0 |
| SMS shipping notifications | 342 | 0 | $0 |
| **Total** | **14,316** | **15** | **$1,092.08** |

For a store this size, email should be doing meaningful double-digit percentages of revenue.
It is currently doing roughly nothing — and the reason is not the copy. **Three things are
actually broken, one flow is actively damaging the account, and the highest-value automations
were never built.**

The good news: the Welcome Series works. 32.4% open rate, $0.60 per recipient, $78.91 AOV.
That single flow produced 79% of all Klaviyo revenue from 10% of the sends. It proves the
list responds when the mechanics are right.

## 🔴 Critical — fix this week

### 1. The Abandoned Cart flow is damaging your sending reputation

This is the most urgent item in the account.

| Metric | Abandoned Cart | Welcome Series | Read |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recipients | 6,187 | 1,475 | AC is 77% of flow volume |
| Open rate | **5.2%** | 32.4% | 6× gap on the same subscriber base |
| Click rate | **0.20%** | 1.52% | Effectively nobody clicks |
| Bounce rate | **12.77%** | 2.17% | 790 bounces |
| Revenue/recipient | **$0.031** | $0.60 | 19× gap |
| Revenue | $166.07 | $868.03 | 77% of sends → 16% of revenue |

One message inside the flow (`SbKzEd`) is the worst offender: **695 bounces out of 1,798
sends — a 38.65% bounce rate, 61% delivery.** It also drew the account's only 2 spam complaints.

A 12.77% flow bounce rate is the kind of number that gets an account throttled or suspended
by Klaviyo, and it drags down inbox placement for *every other email you send* — including
the Welcome Series that actually works. The 6× open-rate gap between two flows mailing
overlapping people is the tell: this is not an audience problem, it's a configuration problem.

**Do this:** pause the flow today. Then check three things before relaunching — whether it
triggers on "Added to Cart" rather than "Checkout Started" (Added to Cart catches bots and
window-shoppers), whether it has an exclusion for people who already purchased, and where
message `SbKzEd`'s recipient list is coming from. Relaunch only when bounce is under 2%.

### 2. You have no authenticated sending domain

`get_sending_domains` returns empty. Every email is going out on Klaviyo's shared
infrastructure rather than an authenticated `jurassicapparel.com` domain with your own
DKIM and SPF records.

Since the 2024 Google and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements, this alone caps your inbox
placement — and it means your reputation is pooled with every other sender on that shared
resource. It is a one-time DNS setup and it is the single highest-leverage deliverability
fix available.

### 3. You are mailing lists you should not be mailing

Every campaign in the account has `excluded: []` — **no suppression, ever.** Worse, the
audience selection is picking up lists that shouldn't receive marketing email:

- The **Nov 2025 campaign** was sent to *Preview List*, *SMS Subscribers*, *Newsletter* and
  *Old List Upload* — all four. "SMS Subscribers" is a phone list; "Preview List" is
  Klaviyo's internal default.
- **Old List Upload** appears in 5 campaigns. The March 2026 campaign that included it ran a
  **20.87% bounce rate**. That is the signature of a stale or cold uploaded list.

Every send to a dead address is a direct withdrawal from your sender reputation.

## 🟠 Broken — high value, currently producing nothing

### 4. Browse Abandonment fired 15 times in a year

Fifteen. On a site pulling ~1,100+ organic sessions a month before any other traffic source.
This flow should be triggering hundreds of times a year.

It opens at 35.7% when it does fire, so the *message* is fine — the trigger or the onsite
tracking is not. Worth noting: your `Viewed Product` metric is coming from the **API
integration, not Shopify**, which is a plausible culprit. Check that Klaviyo's onsite
JavaScript is actually installed and firing on product pages.

### 5. SMS is failing to deliver ~80% of its messages

| Flow | Recipients | Delivered | Delivery rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shipment Confirmed | 101 | 24 | 23.8% |
| Shipment Delivered | 132 | 24 | 18.2% |
| Out for Delivery | 109 | 15 | 13.8% |

Delivery rates in the teens are not a content problem — that pattern almost always means
**A2P 10DLC brand/campaign registration is incomplete or rejected.** Carriers are dropping
the messages before they land.

You also have an SMS Subscribers list and **have never sent a single SMS marketing campaign.**

### 6. Back-in-stock subscriptions are being collected and nothing serves them

The `Subscribed to Back in Stock` metric is live and collecting. There is **no back-in-stock
flow** to notify those people.

This matters more than usual here because the storefront audit found real out-of-stock
inventory — the kids' sneakers, the Mamasaurus tees, several others. Customers are raising
their hands to say "tell me when this is back," and nothing tells them.

| **Sunset / re-engagement** | ❌ Does not exist | |

| **Birthday / VIP** | ❌ Does not exist | |

**The post-purchase gap is the expensive one.** For an apparel brand with a broad catalog —
you have 1,017 best-sellers across 100+ collections — the second order is where the margin
is. Right now a customer buys, gets three shipping notifications, and never hears from you
again in an automated way.

## 🟡 Missing — segmentation

**You have zero segments.** Not "few" — the API returns an empty array.

This is why campaigns underperform. Every send goes to entire lists with no engagement
filtering, which means unengaged and dead addresses get mailed every time, which drives the
bounce and complaint rates, which suppresses inbox placement for the people who *do* want to
hear from you.

Minimum viable set to build:
- Engaged 30 / 60 / 90 day (opened or clicked)
- Customers vs. never-purchased
- VIP (2+ orders, or above-AOV spend)
- Unengaged 180+ days → suppress from campaigns
- Category interest segments (kids vs. adult vs. accessories — your catalog supports this cleanly)

## 🟡 Missing — catalog sync

`get_catalog_items` returns empty. **No products are synced into Klaviyo.**

That rules out dynamic product blocks, personalized recommendations, and "recently viewed"
content in any email. Every campaign has to be hand-built with static images. It also means
the abandoned-cart and browse-abandonment emails can't reliably show the actual product.

## What I'd do, in order

### This week — stop the bleeding
1. **Pause the Abandoned Cart flow.** It is a live deliverability liability.
2. **Authenticate `jurassicapparel.com` as a sending domain.** DNS records, one-time job.
3. **Stop mailing Preview List, SMS Subscribers, and Old List Upload.** Build one "Engaged
   90 day" segment and send only to that until reputation recovers.

### Next two weeks — turn the revenue back on
4. **Rebuild Abandoned Cart properly** — trigger on Checkout Started, exclude purchasers,
   3 messages at 4h / 24h / 72h. Relaunch when bounce is under 2%.
5. **Launch the Winback flow** that has sat in draft for two years.
6. **Build the core segments** listed above.
7. **Restart campaigns at 2–4 per month** to the engaged segment. Given "T-Rex Diet Facts"
   was your best performer, lead with content, not discounts.

### Next month — build what's missing
8. **Post-purchase flow.** Highest-value missing automation: thank-you → care/sizing → review
   request → cross-sell at day 21.
9. **Back-in-stock flow.** Subscriptions are already being collected.
10. **Sync the Shopify catalog** so emails can use dynamic product blocks.
11. **Fix Browse Abandonment** — verify onsite tracking, then let it run.
12. **Sunset flow** so unengaged profiles get retired instead of mailed forever.

### Then — growth
13. **Resolve A2P 10DLC registration**, then use SMS. An 80% failure rate makes the channel
    worthless until this is fixed.
14. **Rebuild the popup**, A/B test the offer, add a second capture point.
15. **Put campaigns on the seasonal calendar we already built for content** — the costume and
    onesie cluster peaks in October, Christmas in Nov/Dec. Email and SEO should hit the same
    windows, not different ones.

---

## The one-line version

The Welcome Series proves this list buys when you reach it properly. Everything else is
either broken, unbuilt, or unsent — and a single misconfigured abandoned-cart flow is
quietly poisoning the deliverability that the rest depends on. Fix that flow, authenticate
the domain, stop mailing dead addresses, and then build the post-purchase flow. In that order.
